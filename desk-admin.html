What the desk login can see

Every tab, link, widget, ticker segment and panel of the Desk Terminal is a switch. Anyone signed in with the desk password sees only what is switched on here. You, signed in with the main password, always see everything. The desk login is an education tool and a macro dashboard, not a signal service, so every row has a class:

analysis shown by default: the bias of the day, risk state, regime, strength, correlations, surprises, forecast ranges. signal trade-like (entries, stops, targets, sizes, best expressions, playbooks, hedge picks): hidden by default. private your own book, record, research, bots and ops: hidden by default.
