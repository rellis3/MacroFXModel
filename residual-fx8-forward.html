What this is

The last lead from the micro bank Mean Reversion of Residuals lesson (48 slides). The lesson's own book failed on our data (q1 FAIL, FX8 + metals NULL). The threshold version on the 8 currencies (enter at |z| ≥ 1.5, exit when z gets back to 0) ran net Sharpe +0.43 / +0.38 in the two backtest halves, but that rule was chosen after looking at the same history. So it is frozen and judged only on data from 2026-11-12 on. Pre-registration: forge/RESIDUAL_FX8_FORWARD_PREREG.md. Paper only: no orders are placed.

  • Every night: PCA on the last 120 days of the 8 currencies' returns (K = 2 frozen) → each currency's residual path → its z-score → threshold trades → inverse-vol weights → factor exposure projected out → gross 1.
  • Costs: house spread on every change in weight, plus OANDA's live overnight financing on each USD pair, logged nightly.
  • Verdicts: F1 (net > 0) is judged on 2028-11-13 and is underpowered by design: at this Sharpe, proof takes years (slide 46). F2 kills the idea if the forward Sharpe's upper bound is below the backtest's 0.38.
