Theory Lab

A from-scratch, math-explained curriculum in the ideas behind quantitative FX/macro research. Every lesson explains a concept the way you'd want it explained if you'd never seen the notation before: plain-English intuition first, then the math step by step with every symbol defined, then a worked numeric example, then how the idea connects to what's actually built in MacroFXModel.

How to read the status badge on each lesson. CONCEPT means the idea is explained here — treat it as background knowledge, not a claim about this project's own build status. Nothing on this page is a trading signal, and nothing here has cleared a real out-of-sample bar just by being explained well — a method is not a strategy until it's tested.
