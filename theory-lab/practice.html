Practice

Reading a lesson once fades within a week. Making decisions with it does not. Each day this page deals you one real, dated market situation from somewhere in the lab. You commit to a call and say how sure you are before you see what happened. Over time it shows you two things a P&L takes years to show: which ideas you can actually use, and whether your confidence can be trusted.
