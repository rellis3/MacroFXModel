Learning Paths

The lab has more than 160 lessons. You don't need all of them. Each path below is a route through the ones that matter for one goal: reading volatility, reading the macro board, building a trading system, or running a portfolio's risk. Every path shares the same core, because the basics of probability, honest testing and your own psychology apply to all of them. Each path ends with a capstone: something concrete you build, not a quiz.

How progress works. A lesson counts as done once you've read 90% of it, either the full lesson or its visual guide. Your chosen path and your capstone ticks are saved in this browser; to carry them to another device, use Sync across devices below. Once you pick a path, every lesson on it shows where you are and what comes next.
