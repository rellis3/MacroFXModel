Theory Lab

Plain-English lessons on how markets, rates, currencies and options work. Each one starts with the intuition, then shows the maths step by step with every symbol defined, then works a real example, then how the idea connects to what's actually built in MacroFXModel. If you want an order, follow a learning path. If you already know what you're after, go straight to the lessons.

How to read the status badge on each lesson. CONCEPT means the idea is explained here — treat it as background knowledge, not a claim about this project's own build status. Nothing in the Theory Lab is a trading signal, and nothing here has cleared a real out-of-sample bar just by being explained well — a method is not a strategy until it's tested.
