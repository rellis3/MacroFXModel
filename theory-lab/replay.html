Replay a Real Day

Pick a day that broke traders. You get the briefing a trader had that morning and the chart up to that moment. Then the bars arrive one at a time, and you trade it: buy, sell, or stay out, with a stop if you want one. A stop fills at your level, unless the price opens beyond it or the bar is so wild there were no prices in between: then it fills at the open or the close, so a gap costs what it really cost. At the end you see what happened, what your plan did, and the lessons behind it.
