Reading the Tape

Seven readings, asked over and over from six years of this desk's own board. Every question is a real day: real yields, real gold, real spreads. You commit to an answer before anything is revealed, and if you don't know why the relationship holds, the derivation is one click away — before you answer, not just after.

Not one of these questions asks which way price went. That would be training you to see signal in noise, which is the one thing this desk exists to avoid. Every answer follows from the numbers on the card: which leg of a yield move did it, which gold was trading, what the bond market concluded about an oil move, which link broke. Structure and mechanism, never direction.

This is separate from the one-a-day reading on today.html, and deliberately so. That one is spaced repetition — it asks when you're about to forget, which is what makes getting it right mean something. Practising here never touches that schedule.
