VuManChu-Confirmed Fade — the trader's actual read at the line, tested honestly

The blind mechanical fade (fade every touch of the median/75th) is null. But you don't fade every touch — you fade only when momentum is slowing/turning at the line (VuManChu WaveTrend rolling over / crossing). This wires that in: at each band touch, read WaveTrend causally (bars up to the touch); a confirmed fade = WT overbought/turning-down at an upper band (oversold/turning-up at a lower) — your exhaustion read. Confirmed vs blind, both bands, IS/OOS, honest fills, costed, plus a volatility-of-day split. Honest limits: the exit is a fixed target(open)+stop — it does NOT model your discretionary exit management, so it understates you. A positive confirmed edge that beats blind = the WT read adds real information. A null means the mechanizable core has no edge — not that you don't.
