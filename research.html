What happens next

  • Videos, links, pictures, text wait in the queue below. Ask any Claude session (laptop or phone) to "process the research inbox": the learn-intake skill fetches transcripts (ReelScribe), writes the note, checks every claim against the Evidence Book and turns the ideas into the Director's question cards.
  • PDFs go straight into the Library's reading backlog (the Literature desk reads them). The Library keeps its own password.
  • Ideas come back on Home as question cards; Open a room takes one to the table.

Nothing here calls a model: dropping in is free.
