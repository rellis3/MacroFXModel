Numeric composition, not an AI text read: "Level vs. target" is the current headline YoY% print against the ~2% target every central bank in this universe (Fed/ECB/BoE/RBA/BoC/SNB/RBNZ) targets — positive = running hot (hawkish pressure), negative = running cold (dovish pressure). "Trend" z-scores the current YoY print against its own trailing history, which catches disinflation-from-a-high-base as an improving signal even while the level itself still reads hot (e.g. 9%→6% YoY is still well above target but clearly decelerating). "Core" (ex food & energy) is reported standalone, not blended into the pressure composite — it answers a related but distinct question (is the underlying/persistent pressure hot) rather than adding to the headline read. Core is confirmed for USD/EUR/CHF/CAD/AUD only; GBP/NZD get headline-only coverage until a verified core series ID exists — see the note on each card. AUD/NZD are quarterly at the source (ABS/Stats NZ), not a data gap. Refreshes automatically once/day; source is FRED (needs FRED_KEY on the server).
