COG Hub

Two things live here, both one step removed from the from-scratch math in Theory Lab: applied practitioner-workflow lessons converted from this repo's own raw study notes, and an honest documentation of "COG" — a real trader whose published forecasts this repo reverse-engineered, and the gated trading system that reverse-engineering inspired.

How this differs from Theory Lab. Theory Lab builds ideas up from first principles (Itô's Lemma, GARCH, Kelly). Everything here starts from applied practice or from this repo's own code instead — the workflow of actually reading a forecast, the mechanics of open interest, the architecture of a real gated trading system — and cross-links back to the relevant from-scratch Theory Lab lesson rather than re-deriving the math. Nothing here is a trading signal either: the Course Notes are practitioner frameworks, not validated edge, and COG's own tested result is an honest null (see Lesson 1 below) — see CLAUDE.md's "built ≠ works ≠ has edge" discipline.
