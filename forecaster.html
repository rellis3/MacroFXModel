Forecaster Sheet

The built layers in one place, per instrument: how volatile today is expected to be (layer 3, HAR σ and its regime), the day's range lines (p50 / p75 / p90 of high−low and of the moves up and down from the open), how far price can still travel from now (layer 5, from the latest London checkpoint), and how big a position is for your risk once the stop's real cost in its conditions is allowed for (layer 7). Distances, not direction — no layer has found direction.

σ = expected daily move (HAR-800, % of price). Regime = live estimator σ ÷ HAR σ: above 1.1 the market is running hot versus its long-run level, below 0.9 quiet. Range lines are % of the London-midnight open; a p75 line is passed on about 1 day in 4. Remaining travel: the price levels price can still reach before the session ends, p50 = half the time, p75 = one time in four (21:00 checkpoint flagged unreliable). Size = £ risk ÷ (stop distance × loss given stop), where loss given stop is the p99 loss actually taken when a stop of that size is hit in those conditions (1.0 = the stop fills where it sits; over a weekend ≈ 2×). Units are of the instrument (FX base currency, index points × 1, gold ounces); naive = the size if every stop filled exactly. Indices are provisional for layer 5.
