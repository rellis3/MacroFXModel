What this tracks

The "daily open" setup from the video (@Jay-ColezTrades), run mechanically every night on every instrument we trade, with the exact rules and bid/ask fills the backtest used. Each night the page marks the fib, the order, the fill and the exit, and adds the result to the record below.
1. The open. The first 1-minute bar at or after 17:00 New York (22:00 or 23:00 UK; gold and indices reopen at 18:00 New York).
2. The impulse. Price moves away from the opening extreme by at least 0.10 × ADR (ADR = median range of the last 20 sessions), before 6 hours after the open.
3. The fib. Drawn on the bid. Down move: 1 = top of the opening wick, 0 = the impulse low (it follows new lows until the fill). Up move: mirrored.
4. The trade. Limit at the 1, stop at the 1.2, target the 0 (= 5R). Stop to break-even at +1R. Anything still open at minute 540 is closed. Sells close on the ask, buys on the bid.
