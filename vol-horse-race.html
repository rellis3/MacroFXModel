Vol Horse Race — which σ forecaster wins, per instrument?

The gold workbook's estimator race (HAR-RV won for gold) generalised to every instrument. 8 σ-forecasters — RW (yesterday's realised vol, the floor), EWMA (RiskMetrics λ=0.94), HV (close-to-close, the platform's commodity σ), Yang-Zhang (the platform's FX σ), GARCH (the platform's index σ), HAR-RV (the workhorse), Combo-5050 & Combo-optimal (HAR+GARCH blends) — each scored causally against next-day realised vol, pooled OOS. Ranked on QLIKE (lower = better; punishes under-forecasting vol). This is forecast accuracy, not trading edge — a better σ places the median/75th lines better, it does not itself make the fade profitable. Read: the green cell is the winner for that instrument; the amber-dashed cell is the platform's current σ for that class. If green ≠ amber, the platform is using a beatable estimator there.
