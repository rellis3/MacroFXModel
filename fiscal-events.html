A fiscal event moves markets through supply (how much government debt must be sold, and at which maturities) and credibility (whether the fiscal rules are met, and by how much headroom). The UK documents (the Treasury's Red Book, the OBR's Economic and fiscal outlook, the Chancellor's statement) and the US Treasury's Quarterly Refunding statements are captured as they are published. The numbers in the table are pulled straight from the documents. The written read is a model's reading of the same text. It describes what was announced; it is not a trading signal.
