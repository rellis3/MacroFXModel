Futures Line Research

Everything we have tested on CME futures at the daily volatility forecast lines: how far price travels after each touch (MAE / MFE), what happens where lines stack, whether a median stop or target is hit first, and whether the lines work as an exhaustion point with VWAP as the target.

MAE / MFE at every touch, OH/OL lines. Futures NQ, 6E and GC, every touch of the p50/p75/p90 lines (touch 1, 2, 3, 4+), 30 min to 22:00, by weekday and London hour, against placebo lines. Back vs through splits 50/50 at every touch; the two moves are the same size; lines behave like placebo.

2026-11-17

All line families + stacked lines. Re-run on OC and HL too (18 lines a day) and recorded how close every other line was. Stacks are common (another line within 5 ticks: NQ 17%, GC 67%, 6E 81%). Every family still 50/50. Stacked touches move half as far because they happen on quiet days.

2026-11-17

Better level + stop/target race. In a stack, trade only the line furthest from price. Then stop at the median MAE, target at the median MFE, walked minute by minute: the target comes first 48–50% of the time on every market, family, touch number and window.

Each touch is measured on its own, from the line price at the minute it was touched. Back is how far price came back toward the open (a fade's MFE). Through is how far it pushed past the line (a fade's MAE). For a continuation trade the two swap.

2 · Exhaustion test: fade toward VWAP

The question: if the lines are an exhaustion point, does fading a touch toward the session VWAP pay when price arrives looking spent? Pre-registered before any result (forge/FUTURES_EXHAUSTION_VWAP_PREREG.md).

EntryLimit at the line on the touch minute. Where several lines are touched together, the outermost.
TargetSession VWAP (exchange volume, from 00:00 London) frozen at the minute before the touch. A VWAP that moves with price would fake wins.
Stop0.25σ beyond the line. Out at 22:00 if neither is hit. Scored in R after 2.4–2.8 ticks round-trip cost.
Exhaustion signsMeasured before the touch: stretch from VWAP, range already used, 30-min approach speed, approach volume (rising or fading), repeat touch. Top/bottom third, cut on 2016–22 only.
Show

Every-touch baseline

Why it comes out at zero

VWAP sits about twice the stop distance away (NQ p50: target 142 ticks, stop 105). The fade reaches the target 28–31% of the time and the stop 54%; 15–19% are still open at 22:00. On a random walk the chance of reaching a target twice the stop first is one in three, and here target ÷ (target + stop) = 34–36%. So the bigger payoff is cancelled exactly by the lower hit rate: gross R is −0.004 to −0.013 on all three markets, and cost makes it negative. None of the exhaustion signs tilts the race; the placebo lines and the mirror (continuation) trade come out the same.
