Backtest of the Telegram alerts themselves, not of the raw signal. AnalogML/motif_alert_backtest.py replays motif_track.py / motif_nearing_watch.py's own emission gating bar by bar — only one live touch-run per pair at a time, the provisional refusal, one-nearing-alert-per-run dedup, entry on the bar after confirmation — and races only what would actually have been sent, through the shared pylego.barrier_race walker at the frozen grid (sl 20p, tp 1.5R).

The levers above are the Level Atlas vote-portfolio engine itself, not a copy of it. Concurrency cap, portfolio heat cap, inverse-vol weighting, daily-series construction, drawdown throttle (cliff and graded) and the currency loss gate are imported from js/levelAtlasVoteReview.js; stats come from js/backtestStats.js's portfolioStats and js/fibAtlasVotePortfolio.js's withNonCompoundedDD. Applied in the same order that module's own buildFibAtlasVotePortfolio uses: per-pair cap → heat cap → currency gate → weights → daily series → throttle. No portfolio maths is reimplemented here.

Four vote-atlas levers are deliberately absent because this signal has no equivalent and faking one would be worse than omitting it: Min vote margin (a motif has no vote dimensions), Include p90 rung (no ladder rungs), Fade-stop tightening and Fixed SL fraction / early exit (fade-trade specific — and fibAtlasVotePortfolio.js itself declines to port fade-stop tightening to other engines for exactly this reason, since no equivalent study has been run).

Two stated divergences from live, both measured rather than assumed away. (1) Tick vs bar — the nearing poller sees a live quote every 60s; a backtest sees H1 bars, so a 👀 fires when the bar's whole traded range came within 0.5 × ATR of the level. The strictly-tighter close-only count is exported beside it (—). (2) Confidence panel — replayed with a causal cutoff (only motifs confirmed strictly before the alert bar), which live does not need; a motif confirmed but still open at alert time is excluded from the replayed PF, where live would have raced it to a partial timeout.

A viewer of an already-validated signal, not a new validation — it re-tunes nothing. Sharpe/DD are mark-to-close with no spread variation. Compounded CAGR over ~30k trades is an artifact of fixed-fractional sizing; read Sharpe, max DD and PF.

Does the 📊 panel predict anything? — shown PF vs what actually happened

Each confirmed alert displays a profit factor for its own category on its own pair. Nothing in the live path filters on it. This buckets every trade by the number its own message displayed and grades each bucket by what followed. A panel worth reading should rise left to right. Unaffected by the risk levers above — it is a property of the alerts, not of the sizing.
