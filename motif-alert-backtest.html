Backtest of the Telegram alerts themselves, not of the raw signal. AnalogML/motif_alert_backtest.py replays motif_track.py / motif_nearing_watch.py's own emission gating bar by bar — only one live touch-run per pair at a time, the provisional refusal, one-nearing-alert-per-run dedup, entry on the bar after confirmation — and races only what would actually have been sent, through the shared pylego.barrier_race walker at the frozen grid (sl 20p, tp 1.5R). Portfolio metrics come from js/backtestStats.js's portfolioStats on the combined daily return series — the same method (and the same no-new-metrics rule) the Level Atlas vote portfolio page uses.

Two stated divergences from live, both measured rather than assumed away. (1) Tick vs bar — the nearing poller sees a live quote every 60s; a backtest sees H1 bars, so a 👀 fires when the bar's whole traded range came within 0.5 × ATR of the level. The strictly-tighter close-only count is exported beside it (—), so the size of the approximation is visible. (2) Confidence panel — the 📊 played out · PF · n figures are replayed with a causal cutoff (only motifs confirmed strictly before the alert bar), which live does not need; a motif confirmed but still open at alert time is excluded from the replayed PF, where live would have raced it to a partial timeout.

This is a viewer of an already-validated signal, not a new validation — it re-tunes nothing. Sharpe/DD are mark-to-close with no spread variation, the caveat every pylego.portfolio_sim caller carries. Compounding at a fixed % of ever-growing equity over ~30k trades makes the raw total-return/CAGR figures absurd; read Sharpe, max DD and PF, not CAGR.

Does the 📊 panel predict anything? — shown PF vs what actually happened

Each confirmed alert displays a profit factor for its own category on its own pair. Nothing in the live path filters on it — it is shown to a human and nothing more. This buckets every trade by the number its own message displayed and grades each bucket by what followed. A panel worth reading should rise left to right.
