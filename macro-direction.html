Macro Direction — does macro lead FX drift?

Falsification-first test before any levels or z-exit are built on top. Per pair, per day, it scores a macro direction from the replicated FX-directional factors — carry (2Y rate-differential momentum), real (US 10Y real-yield momentum), risk (VIX momentum, oriented by safe-haven leg) — then measures the forward return in that direction. No fib levels, no z-gate. If macro direction has no edge here, the whole "macro sets direction, levels time entry, z-score exits" bot is built on sand.
⚠️ Built, not proven. Needs a live FRED_KEY + M1 data (Railway). Read the pooled OOS row and the per-factor attribution — a hit rate near 50%, ~0 Spearman correlation, and OOS Sharpe no better than buy-&-hold means macro direction does not lead, and we stop before building entries on it.
