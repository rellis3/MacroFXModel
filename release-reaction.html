Moves are from the instant of the release: SOFR, Euribor and their line in bp of rate (positive = rates up); the 2y and 10y are note futures in % of price, so a negative number there means yields rose; the dollar and Nasdaq in %. The 2y, 10y and dollar are 15-minute CFD or short-history before September 2026 (see the data checks), so their 1- and 5-minute cells are blank for older releases rather than guessed. Agreement counts the SOFR leg, Euribor leg, 2y and 10y that moved in the same rates direction over the first 30 minutes.

Exploratory and descriptive, not pre-registered; it predicts nothing. Built from C.OG's layered frame (Discord, 19 Oct 2026): Layer 1 is what happened, Layers 2 (why) and 3 (how unusual) come next, one at a time. The laptop pushes new bars every 5 minutes around a scheduled release and the page re-reads every 30 seconds, so a release forms here as it happens, at the delay shown in the freshness chip. If the laptop or the Gateway is off, the chip says so and the numbers stop where the data stops. Code: scripts/release_layer1/.
