# Pre-registration — IV-driven forecast ladder vs the production realized-vol ladder

Written 2026-09-23 before `forge/run_vol_iv.py` exists. Frozen.

## Question

Does a forecast ladder whose σ is the option market's 30-day ATM IV (from CME settlements,
`oi_research_book/scripts/14_iv_surface.py`) beat the production ladder (σ chosen from the
7 realized estimators in `forge/vol.ESTIMATORS`) on the thing the ladder is for — the
p50/p75/p90 rungs of H-L, |O-C|, O-H, O-L on the 00:00–22:00 London session?

Prior evidence: IV beat log-HAR for next-bar range on 7/7 (IV_FORECAST_PREREG.md H2).
This test is on the production product itself, not a regression.

## Protocol (mirrors forge/run_vol.py + run_horizons.py exactly)

- Instruments: eurusd, gbpusd, audusd, usdcad, usdchf, usdjpy, nq — the 7 with a CME
  settlement IV history.
- Sessions: `load_daily(..., session="london22")`. Events: ForexFactory calendar,
  per-instrument currencies, as run_vol does.
- `sigma_iv30[t]` = iv30 from the latest settlement date strictly BEFORE session date t
  (settlement ~20:00 UTC on t−1 precedes the 00:00 London open of t). Live equivalent:
  the ~05:00 UTC QuikStrike capture, which shows the prior day's settlement (verified
  2026-09-23: live vs historical iv30 within 0.05–0.19 vol FX, 0.5 vol NQ, corr 0.88–0.98).
- Both ladders fit and scored on the SAME rows (IV coverage, 2020-09 → 2026-09) and the
  SAME 6 expanding walk-forward folds. Realized ladder = `design_vol(train)` (its own
  estimator + width-source selection, as production). IV ladder =
  `design_vol(train, estimators=("iv30",))`. Event multipliers fit on train, applied OOS.

## Pass rule

- **PRIMARY:** OOS combined H-L pinball (the production selection score), summed over
  folds weighted by n, lower for the IV ladder on ≥ 5 of 7 instruments.
- **CALIBRATION GUARD:** pooled over instruments, the IV ladder's mean |exceed − target|
  across all 12 rungs must be no more than 1pp worse than the realized ladder's.
  A sharper ladder that is miscalibrated does not ship.
- Weekly / monthly (5- and 20-session) rungs: reported, OOS exceedance vs target, no
  pass rule (monthly windows overlap; least certain part of any ladder).

## If it passes

Ship as a SEPARATE export (`/api/vol-forecast/iv-ladder/export`), printed by the same
`buildLadderExportText` so the Pine indicators parse it unchanged, with a params file
`js/forecastLadderParamsIV.js` generated by the unmodified `forge/export_ladder_params.py`.
The production ladder is not modified.

## RESULTS (run 2026-09-23) — PASS, modest, not universal

`forge/out_vol_iv/compare.json`. 6 folds each, 2020-09-08 → 2026-08-20. The realized ladder
selected `har_rv_log` in 41 of 42 folds, so IV is up against the best realized estimator.

| Instrument | H-L pinball, IV vs realized | Calib gap IV / realized |
|---|---|---|
| eurusd | −4.1% (better) | 3.6 / 3.3 pp |
| gbpusd | −4.1% (better) | 2.4 / 2.8 pp |
| audusd | −2.5% (better) | 3.2 / 2.8 pp |
| usdcad | −3.1% (better) | 3.1 / 3.2 pp |
| usdchf | −3.1% (better) | 3.0 / 3.2 pp |
| usdjpy | +1.0% (worse) | 4.4 / 2.9 pp |
| nq | +2.0% (worse) | 4.3 / 2.2 pp |

PRIMARY 5/7 → PASS. Calibration guard: IV 3.44pp vs realized 2.94pp (limit +1pp) → PASS.
Weekly/monthly IV rungs: weekly roughly OK, monthly badly off on several (GBP monthly p75
exceeded 6% vs 25%) — not shipped.
