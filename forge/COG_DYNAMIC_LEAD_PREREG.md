# COG-DYNAMIC-LEAD: do yield and futures spreads begin moving first, in identifiable periods, and does FX (or gold, or an index) follow, in a way that can be recognised in real time and traded after costs?

*Pre-registered 2026-10-17, before any spread was related to any asset in this study. Commissioned by the owner: treat the spread-asset relationship as dynamic (periods when it works, weakens, reverses or breaks), not as a static correlation; find when it can be detected without future information; measure the response delay and how consistent it is; test divergence as catch-up versus breakdown; use rigorous walk-forward testing, correction for multiple comparisons and realistic costs; say whether a practical application is supported.*

**What exists and is not repeated.** `analysis/yield_asset_coupling.py` (2026-08-23): daily US yields against 8 indices, 7 FX pairs and gold over 20 years, 972 forward cells: contemporaneous coupling strong (median |IC| 0.17), forward coupling about zero (0.015), best costed cells net Sharpe below 0.5. The ledger's yield-spread sleeve and multi-spread sleeve (US-vs-foreign z-score extremes) lost "validated" status in the 2026-10-17 deflation. COG-CROSS-ASSET (this month) found the US-leg same-hour link is strong and sign-stable for USDJPY, EURUSD and gold but nothing leads. **What is new here:** (1) the relationship is estimated on a rolling basis from completed data only and used as a real-time gate; (2) spreads across maturities and countries, not only the front end; (3) 27 FX pairs, not 7; (4) explicit tests of regime persistence, response delay and divergence; (5) a selection-aware placebo that prices in searching tens of thousands of cells.

## Data (built and audited by `scripts/dynlead/build_panels.py`; audit in `analysis/output/dynlead/panel_audit.md`)
- **Yields, daily (% p.a.)**: US Treasury constant maturity 3M, 1Y, 2Y, 5Y, 10Y, 30Y (FRED H.15); Germany Svensson par yields 1Y, 2Y, 5Y, 10Y, 30Y (Bundesbank BBSIS, listed Federal securities); Japan JGB 2Y, 5Y, 10Y, 30Y (Ministry of Finance); UK 10Y gilt (Bank of England). European short and long end are therefore Bundesbank, not Euribor/ESTR, for the daily study.
- **Assets, daily**: 27 FX pairs (the six majors vs USD plus NZDUSD and all crosses held), gold (XAUUSD), SPX500, US30, US2000, NAS100, DE30, UK100: the last 1-minute OANDA close at or before **17:00 America/New_York** of each trading day (17:00 to 17:00), days with at least 60 minutes of data. **Calendar = the asset dates (about 2 758 days, 2016-01-04 to 2026-08-20).** Nothing after 2026-08-20 is read for any asset (reserved forward block, forge/LOCKBOX_PROTOCOL.md H-A).
- **Timing.** A yield dated t is used as known at the 17:00 New York close of t (H.15 quotes are as of about 15:30 ET; Bundesbank and MoF closes are earlier the same day; the BoE value is that day's). All forward returns start at the 17:00 New York close of date t. Yields are forward-filled for at most 3 business days over holidays; a spread is formed only on dates with both legs.

## Spreads (28 series, fixed)
US-DE at 1Y, 2Y, 5Y, 10Y, 30Y (5); US-JP at 2Y, 5Y, 10Y, 30Y (4); DE-JP at 2Y, 5Y, 10Y, 30Y (4); US-GB 10Y and DE-GB 10Y (2); curve slopes US 10Y-2Y, DE 10Y-2Y, JP 10Y-2Y, US 30Y-10Y, DE 30Y-10Y (5); slope differences (US 10Y-2Y) minus (DE 10Y-2Y) and minus (JP 10Y-2Y) (2); US 2Y minus US 3M (1) = 23 spread-type series, plus five outright controls US2Y, US10Y, DE2Y, DE10Y, JP10Y (5) = **28**. The outright controls show whether a spread adds anything over a single yield.

## Definitions (all causal)
- **Spread move** x_{j,k}(t) = (S_j(t) - S_j(t-k)) / (sigma_j(t) sqrt(k)), k in {1, 5, 20} trading days, sigma_j(t) = standard deviation of S_j's 1-day changes over the previous 250 days (at least 120), excluding day t.
- **Asset response** y_{a,h}(t) = log P_a(t+h) - log P_a(t), h in {1, 2, 5, 10, 20} trading days.
- **Relationship state** IC_roll(t) = correlation of x_{j,k}(s) with y_{a,h}(s) over the W most recent *completed* pairs, s <= t - h (the pair's return window has ended by t), W in {250, 500} days. No pair whose outcome is not yet known at t is ever used.
- **Gate**: the relationship is "active" at t if |IC_roll(t)| >= 2 / sqrt(W / h) (about 2 standard errors with effective sample W/h); sign = sign(IC_roll). Otherwise the state is "off".
- **Position**: pos(t) = sign(IC_roll(t)) x clip(x_{j,k}(t), -2, 2) / 2 when active, 0 when off. Holding period h days, implemented as the average of the last h daily positions applied to the next day's return (staggered tranches). Ungated comparison: always on with the sign of IC_roll.
- **Costs** (round trip, in bp of notional, charged on |change in position| at half per side): USD majors (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, USDCHF, USDCAD, AUDUSD, NZDUSD) 0.8; EURGBP, EURJPY, EURCHF 1.2; all other crosses 2.0; gold 2.5; indices 1.5. No financing / swap points are charged or earned (stated limitation: a spread signal can be a carry proxy and carry is not in these returns).
- **Net Sharpe** = mean daily net P&L / std x sqrt(252); t-statistics use Newey-West with lag h + 4.

## Splits (chronological; a one-year warm-up, 2016, gives the first rolling estimates)
Discovery 2017-01-01 to 2020-12-31; Validation 2021-01-01 to 2023-12-31; **out-of-sample 2024-01-01 to 2026-08-20** (2.6 years). A cell with W = 500 has no estimate before late 2017 and is evaluated only from its first available date.

## Test families (separate corrections; each cell is spread x asset x k x h x W)
**Family M (momentum / lead): 28 spreads x 34 assets x 3 k x 5 h x 2 W = 28 560 cells**, gated, as defined above.
**Family D (divergence / catch-up)**: divergence e(t) = r_k(t) - beta_roll(t-1) x Delta_k S(t), where r_k is the asset's k-day return, Delta_k S the k-day spread change, beta_roll the rolling contemporaneous regression slope of r_k on Delta_k S over the previous W = 250 days, scaled by the rolling standard deviation of e; **catch-up** position = -sign(e) x clip(e/sigma_e, -2, 2) / 2 held h days; k in {5, 20}, h in {5, 10, 20}; state = rolling contemporaneous R-squared over the previous 250 days, "high" if in the top third of its own trailing 500-day distribution (a strong relationship: catch-up expected) and "low" otherwise (relationship broken: divergence expected to persist); the same position is scored in each state separately. 23 spreads x 34 assets x 2 x 3 x 2 states = 9 384 cells.

## Selection and testing protocol (the overfitting guard)
1. **Step 1, Discovery**: every cell's net Sharpe and one-sided Newey-West p (mean > 0); Benjamini-Hochberg 10% across the family.
2. **Step 2, Validation**: Discovery survivors only; a survivor continues if net Sharpe > 0 and one-sided p < 0.05 on Validation after BH 10% across the survivors.
3. **Step 3, out-of-sample**: Validation survivors are frozen and scored **once** on 2024-01-01 to 2026-08-20.
4. **Selection-aware placebo** (the global test): each spread series is circularly shifted by an independent random number of trading days between 750 and the sample length (the relationship's timing is destroyed, each spread's own persistence is kept) and Steps 1 to 3 are rerun in full; **40 draws** per family. The real count of frozen out-of-sample passes (net Sharpe > 0 and p < 0.05) and the real best out-of-sample net Sharpe must exceed the 95th percentile of the draws.

## Descriptive dynamic questions (reported for every family, whatever the outcome)
- **D1 lead-lag structure**: cross-correlation of the daily spread move with the asset's daily return at lags 1 to 20 for each spread-asset pair, per period; the lag of the largest |correlation| and whether it repeats across Discovery, Validation and out-of-sample (typical delay and its consistency); the share of lags significant after BH versus the shifted placebo.
- **D2 regime persistence (real-time detectability)**: for every cell the correlation between IC_roll(t) (known at t) and the realised relationship over the next W/2 completed pairs (not known at t), on non-overlapping dates; distribution of those correlations versus the shifted placebo. A relationship cannot be recognised before the move unless this exceeds the placebo.
- **D3 regime shape**: share of time active-positive / active-negative / off, mean duration of an active spell, and how often an active-positive state is followed by active-negative within 250 days (reversal).
- **D4 response delay while active**: the signed mean of y_{a,h} over h = 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 days when active, versus off; the delay at which half of the 20-day cumulative response has occurred, and the spread of that delay across calendar years.
- **D5 failure rate**: among active activations that end with a net loss over their holding period, the share, and the share of active spells whose sign reverses.

## Nonlinear check (small, fixed)
Boosted trees (the COG-SHAPE-AGNOSTIC hyper-parameters) for five assets (EURUSD, USDJPY, GBPUSD, GOLD, SPX500), features = x at k = 1, 5, 20 for four spreads (US-DE 2Y, US-DE 10Y, US-JP 10Y, US 10Y-2Y), IC_roll states, divergence z, and the asset's own trailing returns and volatility; target = sign and size of the next 5-day return; **walk-forward by calendar year** from 2019; baseline = asset-only features; IC gain with day-block bootstrap and 30 shifted-spread placebo draws; economic gate as below. It cannot add a finding that Families M and D lack; it only checks that gating was not hiding a nonlinear effect.

## Practical-application gate (all must hold for "supports a practical application")
A frozen out-of-sample cell (or, for a portfolio of frozen cells, their equal-risk combination) with: (a) net Sharpe >= 0.5 after the costs above and one-sided p < 0.05; (b) selection-aware placebo p < 0.05 for the family; (c) positive net return in at least 2 of the 3 out-of-sample calendar years (2024, 2025, 2026 to August); (d) still positive at twice the cost; (e) the same sign for at least one further frozen cell sharing the asset or the spread (consistency). Otherwise the verdict is: not a practical application (context only, or nothing).

## Stage B (intraday futures spreads, registered now so that it cannot depend on Stage A)
Lines: the 15-minute SOFR / Euribor / ESTR futures spreads of COG-MATCHED-PAIRS (P0 to P4) and the US leg alone; bars as there (close-labelled, window to 2026-08-20); assets: the same 34 (OANDA 1-minute caches); x changes over k in {4, 16, 96} bars, y horizons h in {4, 16, 96, 192} bars (1 h, 4 h, 1 day, 2 days), W in {20, 60} trading days of completed pairs, same gate rule with effective sample W x 96 / h bars **capped at the number of days** (day-clustered), same position, same costs per class; splits Discovery 2024-05-01 to 2025-03-31, Validation 2025-04-01 to 2025-12-31, out-of-sample 2026-01-01 to 2026-08-20; 6 lines x 34 assets x 3 x 4 x 2 = 4 896 cells; same protocol and a shifted-line placebo (draws shifted 5 to 60 days); same practical-application gate (out-of-sample years replaced by the two out-of-sample quarters being positive).

## Reporting
One document with: data audit; Family M, Family D, Stage B and the nonlinear check each with Step 1/2/3 counts; the strongest frozen combinations (spread, asset, k, h, W, state), the conditions in which they work (period, volatility, rolling-state), the typical and the consistent response delay, how often the relationship fails (D5), regime persistence (D2) against placebo, and the verdict on practical use. Anything promising that fails a step is recorded as exploratory with the step it failed. **No rule above is changed after results.** If the answer is "no", that is the answer.

## Priors stated before looking
The earlier daily study found real same-bar coupling and essentially no forward information; the deflated sleeves suggest any edge there was thin. The expected outcome is a negative or a conditional-context result. The study is built so that a genuine dynamic effect would be found if it exists, not to find one.
