# COG-SEQUENCE-EVENTS: an interpretable test of the sequence "direction change, then confirmation / confluence, then Nasdaq after a lag", read alongside the flexible model

*Pre-registered 2026-10-15, before any sequence was detected. Companion to COG-SHAPE-AGNOSTIC (registered earlier the same day; its run was in progress and its result unread when this was written). The owner's instruction: keep an interpretable event-based approach next to the flexible model so we know what it detects; look for repeatable **sequences** that may take different shapes; establish when the confirmation becomes available and what Nasdaq does afterwards; strict chronological out-of-sample testing; Nasdaq-only baseline and placebo controls; do not force a result. This file adds three things: (A) a sequence state machine with a pre-fixed family of ways to confirm, (B) a selection-aware placebo, because the main overfitting risk of a spec grid is picking the best of many, and (C) diagnostics that tie the interpretable events to the flexible model and guard that model against memorisation.*

## Series and clocks (unchanged)
Research line `x` (bp, 15-minute, close-labelled, raw, no display transform, no plot lead), Nasdaq `y` (log). Decision time = the close of the bar on which the sequence's last stage completes; every Nasdaq outcome starts at that bar's close; features use data to and including that bar. Window 2024-04-15 to 2026-10-09. Splits chronological: in-sample 2024-04-15 to 2025-03-31 (all selection happens here), validation 2025-04-01 to 2025-12-31, out-of-sample 2026-01-01 to 2026-10-09. The video week is shown separately and excluded from every pooled statistic. Honest limit (as before): no period is untouched for the combined line; the forward record from 2026-10-15 is the untouched test.

## (A) The sequence state machine (causal; theta = the line's 20-calendar-day RMS of 4-hour changes, as everywhere)
**Stage 1, direction change (candidate).** Track the line's running extreme. A candidate turn exists when the line has moved at least **k1 x theta** away from that extreme in the opposite direction; the extreme bar is the **occurrence time** e, the bar of the retrace is the **trigger time** t1 (information available from t1, never from e).
**Stage 2, confirmation / confluence**, within **W bars** of t1 and while the new leg has not given back past the extreme e (past e = sequence cancelled). Six confirmation types, each a different "guise":
1. **continue(c)**: the line extends at least c x theta beyond its level at t1, in the new direction (a second push), c in {0.5, 1.0};
2. **retest(r)**: after the first push the line pulls back by at least r of the first push (r in {0.3, 0.5}) without reaching e, then makes a new leg high/low beyond the first push (a higher low / lower high that holds, then resumes);
3. **accelerate(s)**: the 4-bar change in the new direction is at least s x theta (the speed of the move, not its size), s in {0.5, 1.0}.
The bar on which stage 2 is first satisfied is the **confirmation time t2**: this, not e or t1, is when a signal exists.
Grid: k1 in {0.25, 0.5} x the six confirmation types x W in {8, 16, 32} bars = **36 specs**. **A-priori primary spec (fixed now): k1 = 0.5, continue(0.5), W = 16.** Up and down sequences are pooled with the outcome signed by sequence direction and reported separately for the primary spec.
**Stage 3, Nasdaq after a lag.** From t2's close: signed log return in the lag buckets (0, 4], (4, 8], (8, 16], (16, 32], (32, 64] bars (1 h ... 16 h), and cumulative over H in {4, 8, 16, 32}; **response time** = bars until Nasdaq first moves +u in the sequence direction (u = 0.75 x Nasdaq's 20-day RMS of 4 h changes), as a share responding within 64 bars and the median time; **response vs adverse race** (+u first / -u first / neither within H = 16); log absolute move over the next 8 bars (volatility).
**Context strata** (Nasdaq at t2): all; Nasdaq's own zig-zag (k 0.75) trend opposed to the sequence; Nasdaq consolidating (8-bar range at or below its previous-20-day 30th percentile); and **Nasdaq has not yet shown its own sequence** (no Nasdaq stage-2 confirmation in the same direction, same primary spec on Nasdaq's own path, in the previous 32 bars). The last is the lag claim itself: the line moves first and Nasdaq has not.

## Baselines and controls
1. **Nasdaq-only matched controls** (Amendment B rule: exclusion only around the event's own bar window): 20 random bars matched on Nasdaq zig-zag state, hour +/-1, trailing 1 h return quintile and trailing 2 h range quintile. Effect = event minus matched mean; **day-block bootstrap** (2 000) for CIs and one-sided p.
2. **Nasdaq's own sequence**: the identical state machine on Nasdaq's path (primary spec); effects of line sequences are reported with and without Nasdaq's own sequence active, to show whether the line adds anything a Nasdaq-only sequence does not.
3. **Nasdaq-only regression**: forward return and log |move| on Nasdaq-only features (previous 1 h, 4 h, 24 h returns, trailing 8/16-bar range, log 20-day theta, 96-bar range position, own zig-zag state, hour dummies) plus the sequence signal (primary spec, +/-1 for 8 bars from t2); date-clustered SE; out-of-sample R-squared with / without.
4. **Time-shift placebo (plain)**: line shifted by 5-60 whole days (200 draws), full pipeline rerun on the primary spec; the placebo mean of every matched effect must be near zero (a mean more than 0.02 from zero in share metrics voids the matched comparison for that metric, as in Amendment B).
5. **Selection-aware placebo (the overfitting guard)**: for each of 60 shifted-line draws, run **all 36 specs in-sample**, pick the spec with the best in-sample t (H = 8 cumulative signed return, unmatched, hour-of-day demeaned for speed), and score that pick on validation and out-of-sample. The real "best of 36 in-sample, scored out of sample" result must exceed the 95th percentile of this null. This prices in the freedom of choosing among 36 definitions.

## Selection protocol
Only in-sample data may rank specs. Frozen for evaluation: the a-priori primary spec **and** the in-sample top 3 by t at H = 8 that also pass BH-FDR 10% across the 36 x 3 contexts x 4 horizons in-sample family (if fewer than 3 pass, only those that pass; if none, only the primary). Each frozen spec is then scored on validation and out-of-sample **once**.

## Pass rules (a "repeatable sequence")
A frozen spec (or the primary) counts only if, in **both** validation and out-of-sample: matched effect on the H = 8 signed return above zero with one-sided p < 0.05 **and** the favourable-minus-adverse race share above zero; **and** the plain placebo mean near zero and the real effect above its 95th percentile; **and** the selection-aware placebo test passed (for in-sample-selected specs); **and** the sequence signal's incremental coefficient in the Nasdaq-only regression has t > 2 in both halves with out-of-sample R-squared not lower; **and** not removed by excluding 30-minute windows after 08:30 / 10:00 ET releases and FOMC decisions. Costs: expected signed 2 h return per event net of 0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0 bp round trip with a day-clustered 95% CI; a statistically passing sequence whose net return at 1.0 bp has a lower bound at or below zero is labelled **context only (no trade)**. **Volatility** and **response-time** findings are judged on the same gates against their own matched baselines and labelled separately from direction. Nothing found outside the frozen specs, and nothing in the lag-bucket profile, can promote a finding; those are descriptive.

## (C) Tying the interpretable events to the flexible model, and guarding it
Using the walk-forward predictions of COG-SHAPE-AGNOSTIC (M0 Nasdaq-only, M1 with the line; rerun inside this study with identical code and seeds):
- **C1, where the model's edge sits**: out-of-sample mean of sign(M1 - M0) x forward return, inside the 32 bars after any stage-2 confirmation (union of the 36 specs) versus outside, with a day-block bootstrap CI on the difference; and the correlation of (M1 - M0) with the primary sequence signal. If the model's edge is not concentrated where sequences are, the interpretable events are not what it detects.
- **C2, surrogate**: a depth-3 decision tree (at least 500 rows per leaf) fitted on validation-period (M1 - M0) from the line features only, scored on out-of-sample (M1 - M0) (fidelity R-squared), with its rules printed in plain terms.
- **C3, stability**: the IC gain by quarter for the two primary targets (dir 4 h, vol), counting quarters with a positive gain; a gain carried by one quarter is flagged.
- **C4, complexity ablation (reporting only, nothing selected from it)**: the same walk-forward with (a) a ridge regression on the same features (standardised, median-imputed), (b) trees of depth 2 with 30 iterations, (c) only the zig-zag / confluence / momentum features. If only the deepest model gains while simple versions do not, the gain is flagged as memorisation-prone.

## Verdict wording
One of: **repeatable sequence found** (a frozen spec passes all gates; the flexible model's gain, if any, is reported as consistent or not with it by C1-C3); **conditional / context only** (passes statistics but not cost, or only in one stratum after FDR); **no repeatable sequence** (nothing passes; this is a valid and expected outcome). The flexible model and the sequence study are reported side by side whatever each says.
