# COG-DYNAMIC-LEAD, Addendum C: spread-move episodes with a confirmation that the asset is following

*Registered 2026-10-17 **after** families M and D, stage B and the descriptive dynamics returned (all negative; see below) and after the owner restated the idea: the relationship will not track all the time; there will be periods when it does; **the asset starting to follow the spread is itself the confirmation** that the relationship is live in that episode, and that confirmation is what can be acted on. This is a new hypothesis, not a rescue of the rolling-gate result: the rolling gate uses a 250-day estimate of a *forward* relationship (which the descriptive study found to anti-persist), whereas this tests an *episode* that is recognised from the asset's own early response, in the sequence C.OG's video uses (move, confirmation, then the rest of the move). Disclosure: the specifications below were fixed after seeing M / D / B, but before any episode was detected.*

## What the earlier results said (the reason for a different design)
Family M (28 560 cells), family D (9 384) and stage B (4 896 intraday cells): no cell survives Discovery after correction; the real count equals the placebo count (zero). Same-day link strong (median |corr| 0.10), forward link about 0.025; Discovery lead-lag peaks do not repeat (84 BH-significant in Discovery, 0 in validation, 0 out of sample); the relationship state anti-persists (pooled correlation of the trailing state with the next-125-day state of -0.04 to -0.07, below the placebo mean, placebo p95 +0.02), so a slow rolling estimate cannot recognise it in time; active states cover only 0.6 to 4% of the time.

## Episode definition (daily, all causal; same panels, window, splits and costs as COG-DYNAMIC-LEAD)
1. **Spread move (event)**: day t0 on which |x_{j,k}(t0)| >= z0 for spread j (z-scored k-day change as defined in the main registration), with a 10-day cooldown per spread. Grid: z0 in {1.0, 1.5, 2.0}; k in {1, 5}.
2. **Expected direction for asset a**: d = sign(beta_{j,a}(t0)) x sign(x_{j,k}(t0)), where beta is the rolling **contemporaneous** regression slope of the asset's daily return on the spread's daily change over the previous 250 days (data to t0 - 1 only). The same-day link is the one stable relationship found (EURUSD, USDJPY, gold with the US leg), so the sign of the episode is taken from it, not from a forward estimate.
3. **Confirmation (the asset starts to follow)**: after t0, over the next m days, the asset's log move in direction d is at least c of its own typical move: d x (log P(t0+m) - log P(t0)) / (sigma_a(t0) x sqrt(m)) >= c, sigma_a = standard deviation of the asset's daily returns over the previous 250 days. Grid: m in {1, 2, 3, 5}; c in {0.25, 0.5, 1.0}. The confirmation is known at the close of t_c = t0 + m; **nothing before t_c is used to enter**.
4. **Trade**: enter at the 17:00 New York close of t_c in direction d, hold h days, h in {2, 5, 10, 20}; **net** = d x (log P(t_c + h) - log P(t_c)) minus the round-trip cost of the asset class (as in the main registration).
5. **Coupling state** (the "periods when it works"): either **any**, or **coupled**: the rolling contemporaneous R-squared of the asset on the spread over the previous 60 days (data to t0 - 1) is at least 0.10 **and** the sign of the 60-day slope equals the sign of the 250-day slope. A 60-day window is used because the same-day relationship is strong enough to be estimated quickly, unlike the forward one.
Specifications: 3 (z0) x 2 (k) x 4 (m) x 3 (c) x 4 (h) x 2 (state) = **576 specs**, each pooled over all spreads (28) and assets (34) events.

## Statistic, selection and the null
- Per spec and period (by the decision date t_c): mean net signed return of confirmed episodes in bp, with a one-sided t-statistic (mean > 0) from standard errors **clustered by calendar week** (episodes on the same date across spreads and assets are one cluster, so this is a conservative effective sample).
- **Discovery 2017-01-01 to 2020-12-31 -> Validation 2021-01-01 to 2023-12-31 -> out-of-sample 2024-01-01 to 2026-08-20**: Step 1 BH-FDR 10% across the 608 specs with mean > 0; Step 2 survivors need mean > 0, p < 0.05 and BH 10% across survivors; Step 3 frozen survivors scored once out of sample (mean > 0 and p < 0.05).
- **The null that matters is not zero.** If the asset has already moved in direction d for m days, ordinary momentum alone may make it continue, whatever the spread did. The selection-aware placebo therefore keeps the whole procedure and replaces each spread with a circularly shifted copy (independent random shifts of 750 days or more, as before; 200 draws): shifted episodes have random timing and a random expected direction, so any continuation that appears after a confirmed move without a real spread link (momentum, drift, the confirmation rule itself) appears in the placebo too. A real result must exceed the placebo's distribution of (a) the number of frozen out-of-sample passes (95th percentile) and (b) the pooled out-of-sample mean of the best frozen spec (95th percentile). Individual specs are also compared with the placebo mean for the same spec (excess over the momentum baseline).
- **Failure rate**: for every frozen spec, the share of confirmed episodes with a negative net return, per year.

## Questions this must answer, in the owner's terms
- **Does confirmation mark periods when the relationship is live?** Mean net return of confirmed episodes against episodes of the same spread moves where the asset did **not** follow (same events, same direction rule, different early response), and against the placebo.
- **Response delay and its consistency**: the average signed asset response by day L = 1 to 20 after t0, for all events, for coupled events and for confirmed ones, per period and per calendar year; the day by which half of the 20-day response has occurred and its spread across years.
- **Can the coupling state be recognised in real time?** By construction both the 60-day state and the confirmation use only data to the decision date; the test is whether the *later* return differs (coupled versus not, confirmed versus not) beyond the placebo.
- **Divergence**: events where the spread moved and the asset moved the **opposite** way over the m days (cr <= -c) are tested as catch-up (trade d) and as breakdown (trade the asset's own move); reported separately, same protocol, **32** further specs (m in {1,2,3,5} x c in {0.5, 1.0} x h in {5, 10} = 16 at z0 = 1.5, k = 5, state any, each scored with both trades) counted in the same family (the family is therefore 576 + 32 = 608). ## Practical-application gate (all of)
A frozen out-of-sample spec with: net Sharpe-equivalent of its episode returns >= 0.5 after costs (annualised from the per-episode mean and standard deviation and the episode frequency, stated), one-sided p < 0.05, the placebo gates (a) and (b) above, positive mean in at least 2 of the 3 out-of-sample years, positive at double cost, and at least one further frozen spec sharing the asset or the spread with the same sign.
