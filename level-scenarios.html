What this shows. For every level on the chart, the chance price reaches it within 30 min, 1 h, 2 h, 4 h and by 22:00 London, and for the nearest level above vs below, which is touched first. Reach comes from the Level Engine Phase 2 model (frozen, fitted before 2022): distance in σ over the vol-time left in the day, range used, which side price has moved to, the hour and the asset class. Validated 2022–2026: Brier skill over plain geometry +1.7% (by 22:00) to +5.4% (30 min), calibration within 1pp. Which-first is the distance ratio b ÷ (a + b); nothing beat it. Where a level came from does not change its reach chance (tested across 13 level families), so pivots, fibs, round numbers and forecast lines are all scored by the same rule. What happens after the touch is ~50/50: rejection vs continuation has no validated edge (every level family tested null), so this page never gives a direction.
