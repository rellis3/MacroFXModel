When options price more movement than the lines, the lines break

Each Vol Forecast line is a quantile of the day's range, built from past price alone. The surface shows how often the range actually went past each line minus how often it should have, split by how rich options were against the forecast that morning. Flat grey means the lines are fair. Orange means the lines were too tight and breaks continued. Blue means the lines were too wide and price stalled before reaching them.
