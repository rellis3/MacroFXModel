Forecast Interval Coverage · does HL75 actually hold 75% of days?

The forecast bands promise frequencies: realized range (high−low) should stay inside hl50 on ~50% of days and inside hl75 on ~75%; |close−open| likewise vs ocMed/oc75. This card measures exactly that — no lookahead (day i's σ uses bars < i), the band math imported from forecastCore.js (never copied), deviation sized in binomial standard errors, split by year so a drifted ruler can't hide in the full-sample average. Plus a GPD read (extremesCore) on how hard hl75 breaks when it breaks. Measurement, not edge — this grades the ruler.

Reading it: cov is the observed frequency; the nominal target is 50% / 75% by band. z = (observed − nominal) / binomial SE — |z| ≤ 2 calibrated, 2 < |z| ≤ 3 suspect, |z| > 3 miscalibrated. med(rng/HL50) should sit ≈ 1.00 — above 1 the bands run tight (too narrow), below 1 they run wide. break % = share of days the range escapes hl75 (nominal 25%); mean exc = average overshoot beyond hl75 when it breaks; ξ is the GPD shape of those overshoots — ξ > 0 means band breaks are fat-tailed (the typical break understates the bad ones). Click a row for the per-year split — a healthy full-sample 75% that's 85% pre-2020 and 60% after is a drifted ruler, not a calibrated one.
