💱 FX Carry Factor — rate-differential carry, done honestly

Long high-rate currencies, short low-rate ones vs USD, inverse-vol sized, rebalanced, net of cost — with the interest accrual priced in from FRED short rates. IS/OOS. A modest, crash-prone diversifier — not a wealth engine.
Why this exists. A carry return has two parts: spot_return + carry_accrual, where the accrual ≈ (rate_ccy − rate_USD)/252 per day. The older JPY-cross spot proxy used a credit z-score for its signal and measured spot only — it dropped the accrual entirely, i.e. the whole point of carry. This page fixes both: the signal is the rate differential (so it flips when a central bank cuts), and the accrual is added and reported separately so you can see how much edge is spot vs carry.
Honesty caveat. The accrual here is computed at interbank rates (FRED). A retail broker charges a spread on swap, so your real tradeable carry is worse than this backtest — treat it as an interbank upper bound. The financing panel below reconciles it against OANDA's live swap so you can see the actual haircut. Carry is a replicated premium, but it's compensation for crash risk (works for years, then gaps down hard). Not financial advice.
