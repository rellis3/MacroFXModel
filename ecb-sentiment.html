Unlike the Fed, ECB document URLs aren't directly constructible from the date — the fetcher looks them up on the ECB's own index pages, so a fresh release can take slightly longer to appear than the FOMC page's. "Accounts" (the ECB's minutes-equivalent) publish 5+ weeks after the meeting on a date that varies, so its "due" window is wider than a single day.
