Hurst Estimator A/B · is the live range-bias feature a signal or a constant?

The live range-bias conviction score includes a Hurst feature that votes mean-reverting <0.45 / trending >0.55. The incumbent estimator (R/S over lags 2–16, applied to price levels) reads ~0.9 on synthetics regardless of market character. This measures that on real data, and answers the question that actually decides whether to swap it: does either reading predict anything? Forward character is the efficiency ratio (|net move| ÷ path) over the next N days — scored as rank IC with a true IS/OOS split. Benchmark is IC = 0. Being better calibrated does not entitle DFA to win: if both OOS ICs are ≈0, the honest conclusion is that the feature carries no information and should be dropped, not swapped.

dominant vote % is the share taken by the estimator's most common vote. 100% means the feature is a constant — it emits the same verdict every single day, so it adds nothing to the conviction score no matter how sensible the threshold looks. disagree % is how often the two estimators would produce a different vote — the "would swapping actually change the bot" number. OOS IC is the Spearman rank correlation between the reading and the forward efficiency ratio, out of sample; |IC| ≳ 0.2 with a real n is a usable relationship, ≈0 is none.
