Reversion Proof — the median line vs where price actually reverts, every day

Your exact test, made transparent — no aggregate to trust blind. From the London-midnight open, the median line sits a distance R above and below (R = the forecast median H-L range, Feller 1.572×σ, causal). The day's high is where price reverted on the upside, the low on the downside. A win = that reversion landed within your tolerance of the line. Every day is a row you can read and click to see the M1 candles with the line drawn on them. The aggregate is just the average of those rows.
Read this first: R is the full HIGH-TO-LOW range. The open→high distance is only PART of it (the rest is open→low), so a line a full R above the open is expected to sit above where the high lands on a two-sided day. The reach ÷ line stat shows exactly how far short (or past) price reverts — that's the honest answer to "does it revert at the line?"
