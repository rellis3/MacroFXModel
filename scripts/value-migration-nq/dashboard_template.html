NQ Value Migration Study

Does volume-profile value migration (POC, VAH and VAL drifting between 15-minute boxes) predict NASDAQ futures direction, do delta and absorption add to it, and can repeated scalps or a trailing stop turn it into net profit? Every number below comes from the registered run. Nothing was tuned.

Verdicts

What the evidence says, question by question

1 · Prediction

Signed forward move after a migration state

Mean move in the state's direction, in horizon-scaled sigma units (y = move / (σ1·√h)). Above zero means the state pointed the right way. The grey line is the price twin: the same rule on box closes instead of POC. If value migration carried information beyond price, the coloured lines would sit above it.

2 · Strategies

Four models × two entries × three exits, after costs

Net of 1 tick slippage per market fill and $4.50 per round turn. "Random" replays each trade on a random other session at the same minute, side and risk, with the same exit, 20 times. A strategy with no information lands near the random line. Select a row to see its equity and breakdowns.

3 · Regimes

Does the signal behave differently by market condition?

Signed outcome by regime. Ex-ante volatility is knowable before the session. Day type and ex-post volatility use the finished session and are hindsight descriptions, not filters you could trade. Benjamini–Hochberg at q = 0.10 over every slice.

4 · Robustness

Parameter grid, walk-forward and box size

27 grid points (θ 0.25/0.5/1, K 2/4/6, φ 0.25/0.5/1). Walk-forward picks, for each year 2017–2025, the grid point with the best net R over the two previous years. If tuning found something real, walk-forward would beat the fixed default.
