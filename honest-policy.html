Honest-Policy Portfolio — COG's cell-selection, on our honest 1-min fills

COG's "Complete Book" (~+290%, smooth) is not the blind fade — that loses at every resolution. It's the blind fade plus two ingredients: a per-cell selection (learn fade/follow/skip on the in-sample half, trade only the winners OOS) and a multi-pair portfolio. This reproduces both — but keeps the fills honest: real 1-min TP/SL touch order (the shared walkBars), no favourable mark-to-close, no zero-duration trades. It then nets every instrument's OOS stream into one equity curve — the direct analogue of his Complete Book, built honestly. Pre-registered: if this curve is flat/negative, his selection needs the optimistic marking to exist — the fireworks are the fills. If it rises, it's a real conditional edge and we adopt it.
