HAR Shadow Ladder

A side-by-side forward test. Live is the forecast ladder the Vol Forecast publishes today. HAR is a candidate built from the HAR-log σ the forecast already stores each night, with widths fitted on that σ. The live ladder is right on average but tilts with the regime: after quiet spells its p75 is passed on ~30% of days, after busy spells ~19% (target 25%). In the pre-registered test HAR removed most of that tilt. This page watches whether it holds on live data. Nothing here changes the live ladder, the exports or any bot.
