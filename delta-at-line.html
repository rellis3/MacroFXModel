How to read this

Touches: the first touch of each chosen forecast line per session, from the frozen table used by the earlier line research. Reject or break: price moves 0.25 sigma back or beyond within 120 minutes. Flow: the exchange's aggressor flag on 6E for EURUSD and 6J for USDJPY, sign flipped for USDJPY. All flow is from before the touch minute.

Signals: delta against: price moved into the line over 30 minutes with delta in its weakest fifth. Regular divergence: price beyond its last swing high with cumulative delta below that high. Hidden divergence: a higher swing low on lower cumulative delta. Delta with: price into the line with delta in its strongest fifth.

This is a description of past touches, not a signal, and nothing trades on it. Prereg: forge/DELTA_AT_LINE_PREREG.md.
