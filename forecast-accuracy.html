Forecast Accuracy — range accuracy + exhaustion, per calibration

Two lenses on the SAME forecast line — never conflated. Panel A (volatility accuracy): does the forecast predict the day's range MAGNITUDE? Per day, realized H-L / O-C vs the forecast median; "hit" = within ±5% (the colleague's metric). Shown with the exceed rate (realized > median) — the real calibration tell: a well-placed median is exceeded ≈50% of days; <50% ⇒ line runs wide, >50% ⇒ too tight. Three calibrations — Feller (1.57), COG (1.56), recal (~1.34 fx) — vs a naive persistence floor. Panel B (exhaustion / level-fade): where the day's dominant reversal actually lands ÷ the median range (the ~0.65× finding), how often the median line is even touched, and of touches how often price reverts vs blows through. Accuracy of the range ≠ location of the reversal — that's the whole point. ±5% hit rates are low (~15–25%) for any forecast; read them relative, not absolute.
