## Lesson
Two clips from the tier list: linear regression (and its family: multivariate, factor, time-series, pooled,
Fama-MacBeth) is S tier, because "people get rich off of a 0.02 R squared". A tiny R² can be a fortune at scale. The
Bachelier model (Brownian motion, 1900) is S tier for its downstream influence; it was even used in practice when oil went negative (2020).

## What we could learn
- **ALREADY DO**: small effects are judged against cost and power. The R² point is "breadth × tiny edge"; the
  fundamental law (IR ≈ IC × √breadth) is the link. Whether we have the breadth to use small-IC signals is a fair
  question for the portfolio agenda.
