## Lesson
"Did it beat the S&P?" is an ex-post question. A backtest exists to show what you could have known **ex ante**: the
distribution of outcomes you were signing up for. Comparing with a benchmark only after you know how the benchmark did is hindsight.

## Approach
- **Question**: judge a strategy by the outcome distribution it offered at the time, not by the realised path of one alternative.
- **Habit**: keep ex-ante and ex-post reasoning separate.
