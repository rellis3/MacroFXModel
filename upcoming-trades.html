How often do the zones themselves change? Most of this map (pivots, prior day/week hi-lo, fib clusters, round numbers) is built from the last completed daily bar — it only changes once per session, at the next London-midnight rollover (see the As Of column). Two things move more often: today's developing high/low (continuously, on any new intraday extreme) and Asia/Monday confluence (below). The page's own 45s auto-refresh and the NEW tag just surface those — most refreshes reconfirm the same zones, they don't invent new ones.

Asia/Monday zones — confluence only, not the raw grid. The raw Asia/Monday fib ladder (every extension rung of today's session range) is deliberately not shown here — a lone, unconfirmed rung is noise. Only confluence earns a zone: today's Asia ladder agreeing with yesterday's, or this week's Monday agreeing with last week's, within the per-instrument threshold (2 pips FX). Tight agreement (≤10% of that, or the same fib) scores higher. Monday confluence scores higher again than Asia — a weekly level repeating from last week is rarer and more reactive than a daily one repeating from yesterday.
