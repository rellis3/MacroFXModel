Zarattini, Aziz & Barbon (2024) "Beat the Market" — mechanics — P1 — Evidence Book

Strategy

The paper's intraday momentum rule, rebuilt minute by minute on US index futures (ES ≈ SPY, NQ, YM, RTY), cash session 09:30–16:00 New York, Jan 2016 → 21 Aug 2026 (later sessions are the held-out lockbox). Pre-registered before any result was computed: forge/NOISE_AREA_MOMENTUM_PREREG.md.
Noise band
For each minute, the average |move from the open| over the last 14 days. Upper = max(open, prior close)·(1+σ), lower = min(open, prior close)·(1−σ).
Entry
Checked only at :00 and :30 from 10:00. Long above the upper band (and above VWAP), short below the lower band (and below VWAP).
Exit
Paper's improved stop: flat when price is back inside the band or across VWAP at a check. Base version: hold until the opposite band. Always flat at 16:00.
Size
1x notional, or vol-targeted: leverage = min(4, 2% ÷ last-14-day daily σ). Compounds from $100k, fractional contracts.
