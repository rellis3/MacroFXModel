Method & caveats

        

          How every number is made. Each trading day is one experiment. The forecast for day i
          is built from data strictly before i (the same live-forecaster math that draws your chart levels), then scored against what day
          i actually did — the forecast never sees the bar it is graded on. Aggregates only: this book ships distilled statistics, never
          a dump of per-day rows.
          


          Calibration reading. A well-formed median is exceeded ~50% of days and the 75th ~25%.
          Below target ⇒ the band runs too wide (forecast over-estimates); above ⇒ too tight (under-estimates).
          


          Anchoring. The daily/5d/20d evaluation builds its days from intraday grouped by London calendar date — the same London-midnight boundary the live forecaster uses — when H1 is available (falls back to OANDA D1 only if it isn't).
          


          Not yet covered. The news-week / holiday-week stability slices in Q3 (needs an economic-calendar
          feed), the >95th calibration bucket (needs a 95th forecast band), and per-touch outcome sequencing beyond first-vs-retested in the Level-touches
          chapter. All flagged, not faked. Q6 expansion and the level-touch / pip-excursion study are now built (intraday chapters) —
          click ▶ Intraday to populate them.
