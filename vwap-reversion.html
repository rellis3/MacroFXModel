VWAP Reversion Backtester · fade-to-VWAP vs bounce-off-VWAP vs break

Tests whether session VWAP is a tradeable intraday fair-value level — or practitioner folklore. Three modes, one entry primitive (vwapReversionEngine.js), one shared fill walker and one IS/OOS reporter (baseplate, imported). VWAP σ-bands are built from tick-weighted hlc3 — and FX "volume" is tick count, not real traded volume, so this VWAP is weaker than an equity VWAP by construction. Costs are ON.
Pre-registered prior: band-fade ~15–20%, vwap-bounce ~10% chance of a real after-cost OOS edge. Default expectation is null. "Worked" = pooled OOS t > 2 with positive mean and a majority of pairs OOS-positive. A number that's positive only gross, or only in-sample, is not a result.
band_fade = fade the ±σ band back to VWAP (mean-reversion). vwap_bounce = after a stretch, buy/sell the pull-back to VWAP betting it holds (support/resistance). band_follow = break through the band, target the next band out (continuation control). The honest number is the pooled OOS per-trade t-stat (annualisation-free) — a t near 0 is a null; strongly negative means the mode loses the cost every trade. Per-pair OOS Sharpe median and the pairs>0 count guard against one instrument carrying a pooled result.
