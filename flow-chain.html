The course's capital-flow hierarchy (Quant & Macro Insights, Lesson 2.1) says moves travel down a chain, each level lagging the one above: central banks → government bonds (hours–days) → G10 FX (days–weeks) → equities (weeks–months) → credit (months–quarters). Pick a leader and a follower, slide the lag, and watch whether the leader's line, moved forward in time, lines up with the follower. The bar chart shows the correlation of their N-day changes at every lag, with a grey chance band (the same series with the link destroyed). Bars inside the band are noise. Bars in the amber zone overlap in time, so they are the same move, not a lead.
