Derived, not a new data source: 10Y government bond yield minus a short-term/policy-adjacent rate, per currency — reuses series this project already fetches elsewhere. Positive = normal upward-sloping curve (the historical norm — long-term lenders paid more than short-term). Negative = inverted, the classic signal markets are pricing rate cuts / a slowdown ahead. The raw slope is directly comparable across currencies (a percentage-point spread), so it's shown as the primary number; "Trend" z-scores it against its own trailing history — is this currency's curve unusually flat, steep, or inverted relative to its own recent norm. Refreshes automatically once/day; source is FRED (needs FRED_KEY on the server).
