👁 Setup Watch

the educator setups this desk has tested, watched live ·
Each card is a setup an educator (Nicholas Crown, so far) says to act on, with its firing condition frozen from the pre-registration this desk tested it under. The gauge is how far today's reading is along the way to that condition; past the mark it is approaching, at the line it is live. When a setup goes live or starts approaching, the desk-watch Telegram says so once, with the verdict and what to look for attached. Every fire is then scored on the outcome the study pre-registered, at the horizon the study used, and shown here against the number history gave. Most of these are tested null. A live setup is a lesson on the tape, not a signal; nothing here says which way anything goes.
