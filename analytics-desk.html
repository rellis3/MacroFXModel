Reading it: T is trend-day-ness (drift ÷ diffusion) — |T| high = directional day-character. Hurst is DFA on returns (not price levels — levels read ≈H+1 and make everything look "trending"): ≈0.5 independent increments, >0.5 persistent, <0.5 mean-reverting; real series land ~0.4–0.8. Context, and noisy on short windows. OU half-life is how many days a stretch takes to close half the gap to the fitted mean — shown only when the fit says reverting (κ > 0); z is the current stretch in stationary-σ units. Range z is yesterday's range vs its trailing 60 days. Entropy ∈ [0,1]: disorder of the last 60 days' returns; shift is the JS-divergence of the recent window vs the 250 days before it, located as a percentile of this instrument's own history — high percentile = "the distribution has changed", to be read NEXT TO the HMM/day-type regime, not instead of it (the pre-registered A/B).
