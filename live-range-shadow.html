What this adds to Live Range. (1) "High in / low in": the chance the running high (or low) is the day's final one, from the hour, the range used and how far price has already pulled back from that extreme. Walk-forward validated, 2018 → 2026: Brier skill +21% (FX/gold) and +18% (indices) over the hour × used × pace base rate; realised minus predicted within 0.3pp / 1.4pp in every decile. Use it to take profit or stand aside, never as a direction: fading an extreme was tested in many forms and does not pay. (2) Refit lines: the same hourly p75 lines with the grids refit in the page's own σ. The shipped grids were fitted on a σ about 10% lower and sit about 3pp too far out (p75 reached 21.5% of days vs 25% intended); refit lines reach 24.0% (FX) out of sample.
Evidence: forge/LIVE_RANGE_SHADOW_PREREG.md · analysis/output/live_range_shadow/RESULTS_p1.md. The refit file is fitted on all dates (in-sample by construction); the honest numbers above come from the walk-forward. Between hourly checkpoints the read uses the live high, low and price with the last checkpoint's hour, so it is approximate.
