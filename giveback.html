Give-Back — exit quality per bot

For every closed trade: how far it ran into profit (peak / MFE) vs. what the exit actually kept. The gap is the give-back — the open profit handed back before anything banked it. Reconstructed from the real M1 high/low path. ← Dashboard
How to read this. A big give-back on winners = your exits let profit revert (banking / trailing room). But if the losers barely got green (low peak MFE) before reversing, that's an entry/stop problem, not an exit one — banking earlier won't help. Read both per bot before changing anything. The "bank X% of peak" idea is only worth chasing where winners give back a lot and there's a real peak to bank. Covers the KV-logged live bots (Range-Line, OI, Confluence). Gold journals to files — for it use python analysis/bot_giveback.py --bot gold. MFE is reconstructed from the real M1 path on the server.
