How to read this honestly. The cone is the model's real claim: an EWMA drift (capped at ±0.5σ/day) plus Feller close-displacement envelopes from the SAME σ math as the live forecaster (volSigmaSeries → computeBands, imported — never copied). The P50 band claims 50% containment, P75 claims 75%, and drift direction claims to beat a 50% coin flip — the calibration card grades every claim on non-overlapping historical windows. Sample paths are draws from that same claim (wicks are cosmetic); their per-step median is the "most-agreed path". If containment sits near its claim and direction sits at ~50%, that IS the honest result: the model knows range well and direction barely — don't let the picture say otherwise.
