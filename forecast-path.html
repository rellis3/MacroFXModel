How to read this honestly. The cone is the model's real claim: a capped EWMA drift plus uncertainty envelopes. On D1 the envelopes are the Feller close-displacement bands from the SAME σ math as the live forecaster (volSigmaSeries → computeBands, imported — never copied). On M15 σ is per-bar EWMA shaped by a causal hour-of-day profile (Asia quiet, London/NY loud — learned from strictly-prior data), with Gaussian envelopes. The P50 band claims 50% containment, P75 claims 75%, drift direction claims to beat a coin flip — the calibration card grades every claim on non-overlapping windows. Sample paths are draws from that same claim (wicks cosmetic); their per-step median is the "most-agreed path". If containment sits near its claim and direction sits at ~50%, that IS the honest result: the model knows range well and direction barely — don't let the picture say otherwise.
