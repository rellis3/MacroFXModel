COG-level POC — COG's actual levels vs actual price

Paired A/B — COG's forecaster vs ours, on the same days. For every day we have COG reference data, this sets both COG's levels and our forecaster's levels (median / 75th / O-C) and walks that day's intraday price with the SAME measurement: did price reach the level, revert or blow through, by how much. It also reports the width ratio (COG ÷ ours) and whether it's stable — i.e. whether one fixed widening could match COG without weekly tweaking. Median & 75th are the dynamic H-L (projected from the running high/low); O-C is open ± the move. Small N (COG has ~20 days) — directional, not proof. Railway only.
