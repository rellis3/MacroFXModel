COG-level POC — COG's actual levels vs actual price

For every day we have COG reference data, this sets COG's own median / 75th / O-C levels and walks that day's intraday price to answer directly: did price reach the level, did it revert (fade) or blow through, by how much, and how many times. Median & 75th are the dynamic H-L (opposite extreme projected from the running high/low — the "NAS" level); O-C is open ± COG's open-close move. Railway only (reads KV + M1).
