The OI Profile draws one expiry book: the bucket (0–1, 2–7, 8–45 or 46+ days) with the most gamma near price. This page lists every line from every bucket, next to the lines the OI WALLS & MAX PAIN export draws, and says which ones the Profile hides. Strength comes two ways: g is strength inside the line's own book (1.0 is that book's biggest), G is against the biggest gamma anywhere in the market (so a 0–1 day wall with g 1.0 can still be G 0.1, a small line). The levels are rebuilt offline from this morning's QuikStrike capture, with spot and the one-day move taken from what the live Profile printed at 06:47 UTC, and the basis anchored on the live gamma peak. Walls, zero gamma, the strongest book and the regime match the live Profile on the markets I checked (gold, NQ, EURUSD, USDJPY, SPX). Known differences: charm and vanna flips are recomputed offline and sit a little off the live ones (gold 3985 here against 3976 live), and a few weak second walls differ. This describes the book. It is not a signal, and none of it has been tested as one beyond the Level Lab.
