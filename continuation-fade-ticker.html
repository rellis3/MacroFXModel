    How to read this

    WATCHING — price is nowhere near a mapped decision zone; nothing to score.
    APPROACHING — a zone is in reach but price hasn't tested it closely yet (>0.15σ away).
    TESTING — price is inside the zone right now; watch the bar move as new candles close.
    CONFIRMED — one hypothesis crossed the model's go-threshold; the bar shows which and how hard.

    Continuation/Fade % are decisionCore's two independent action:'follow' / action:'fade' probabilities, renormalized to sum to 100% for the bar — the raw (non-renormalized) numbers are shown underneath. Confidence is a separate, uncalibrated heuristic: how far apart the two hypotheses have pulled, blended with snapshot freshness — a 51/49 read is never "confident" even when one side is technically ahead.
