How to read this page

Layer 1 records what happened around each release. This page holds the candidate drivers. Every factor below is known before the release (the last close before it, or the calendar), so it describes the setting and never uses the reaction. Layer 1 numbers are only read here, to show how outcomes differ when a factor is high or low; nothing here changes them. A factor is a hypothesis about a driver, not an explanation, and none has been tested for effect. Shock labels (inflation, growth, policy, risk-off) are read after the move, so they will live in a separate section and never sit on the going-in sheet. Exploratory, not pre-registered.

The research universe: 77 items, what the repo holds for each

Status is my reading of what is on disk or fetchable; I checked the sources named in each note and assumed the rest, so treat "missing" as "not found in the repo", not "does not exist". The list overlaps itself (policy-path items B8 to B15 are largely one thing, the risk-appetite items D25 to D32 largely another), so the sheet carries about thirty measures covering 23 of the 77 items, not 77 separate models.

Descriptive and exploratory. Cut points and ranks use all releases on the sheet, so they describe the past and are not a signal. With so few releases per type (core CPI 25, payrolls 28, Fed decisions 19) many factor-outcome pairs will look interesting by chance: the amber band on each bar is roughly what noise alone produces at that sample size, and with about thirty factors a few bars will cross it purely by luck.
