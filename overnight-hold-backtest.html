Overnight Hold vs Buy & Hold · NAS100 & XAUUSD, then the prop-firm rule check

Enter long 20:00 UK, exit 14:30 UK the following session, M1 data, both instruments trimmed to their overlapping date range. Reports gross and net (spread+slip+overnight financing) against continuous buy & hold over the same window, then runs the net trade series through a configurable prop-firm-style rule check. Source task: education/buy-and-hold-notes.md · engine: js/overnightHoldEngine.js.

Costs, financing and the rule limits below are documented, editable assumptions — not fetched from any live broker/firm feed. Calibrate them to your actual broker's spread/swap and the actual firm's published rulebook before drawing a pass/fail conclusion about either. See the engine file's header for the exact defaults and why each one was chosen.
