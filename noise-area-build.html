The honest backtest: walk-forward

Each January the builder looks only at the years before, picks the design in the middle of the best region of the 1,440 (the plateau: the median of a design and its neighbours in band width, band days and check interval, so a lucky spike can't win), and trades it for that year. Chaining those years gives a record no design ever saw in advance. Grey is the paper's fixed rule on the same days and costs. Compare the Sharpe ratios, not the end balances: when the build picks the vol-targeted size it runs with more leverage than the 1x paper rule.
