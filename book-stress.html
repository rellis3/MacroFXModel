Book Stress · does diversification survive a liquidity contraction?

A book of N sleeves is only N bets if they stay independent. The claim under test (SYSTEM_ASSESSMENT.md §2.4): correlations converge exactly when it matters, so drawdowns that look independent in the full sample arrive together in a crisis. This recomputes the effective number of bets inside each declared crisis window and compares it to the calm-period baseline. Windows are declared constants, not picked after seeing the returns. Risk diagnostic, not a signal — no allocation mode "winning" on past data is evidence it will keep winning.

Effective bets is the inverse participation ratio of the correlation matrix' eigenvalues — N uncorrelated sleeves give N, perfectly correlated sleeves give 1. The number to read is crisis vs calm: if it collapses in the crisis rows, the book's diversification is an illusion precisely when it is needed, and per-sleeve drawdowns should not be treated as independent when sizing. sleeves − counts how many sleeves lost money in that window (all of them = one bet wearing N hats). Uncovered windows are outside the data range and are reported as such rather than filled in.
