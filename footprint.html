Reading it. Each column is one bar. Left number = contracts sold into the bid (aggressive sellers), right = bought at the ask (aggressive buyers). Shading is size. Green / red bold numbers are diagonal imbalances (ask at a price vs bid one row below, at least the chosen ratio and 10 lots); a bar beside three or more in a row marks a stacked imbalance. Amber box = the bar's POC. The right panel is the session volume profile with the 70% value area. Footer rows: volume, delta (buy − sell), and cumulative delta from the session start. Drag to pan, wheel to scroll price, shift+wheel to scroll time.
Feed. scripts/footprint/start_footprint_capture.bat on the NT8 laptop records the front contract of each market, signs each trade against the bid/ask stored with it (the Sign rule tile shows the share; the rest fall back to the tick rule) and sends the day here. Finished days are kept. This page refreshes every 30 s while a day is live and the tab is open. Days built from NT8 tick history use the tick rule, which gets the direction right but understates size. Times are UTC, read from the exchange stamp, and the Lucid feed arrives about 10 minutes late.
Not a signal. Bar-level buy/sell imbalance did not predict the next 5 or 15 minutes over 189 sessions (orderflow-lead, a powered null). Use this to read what happened, not to forecast.
