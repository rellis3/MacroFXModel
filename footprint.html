How to read this

Descriptive only, not a signal. Bar-level buy/sell imbalance did not predict the next 5 or 15 minutes on NQ with the tick rule (orderflow-lead, 189 sessions) or on 6E and 6J with the exchange’s own aggressor flag (orderflow-lead-aggressor, 866 and 780 sessions). Both are powered nulls. Two mechanical absorption readings on 6E and 6J (footprint-absorption) failed their gate and lost about 0.3R a trade after costs, so an amber dot is a description, not a trade. Stacked imbalances have not been tested.

On the chart

  • Footprint cells: contracts sold into the bid on the left, bought at the ask on the right. Stronger shading is more size.
  • Strong cells with white figures: diagonal imbalances, buyers at a price against sellers one row below (or the reverse), at the chosen ratio and at least 10 lots. A bar at the edge of a column marks three or more in a row.
  • Amber: the bar’s busiest price (POC). The dashed amber line is the last price.
  • Delta: buy minus sell per bar, on one scale for the session. An amber dot means delta disagrees with the candle.
  • CVD: the running total of delta from the start of the chosen session. Change the session and it restarts.
  • Session profile: volume at each price, with the 70% value area shaded on the chart.

Moving around

  • Mouse: wheel zooms time, drag pans, shift + wheel pans, double-click fits the session. Drag the price axis to stretch prices.
  • Touch: drag pans, pinch zooms, tap shows the crosshair and figures. Drag the price axis to stretch prices.
  • Keys: ← → pan, + − zoom, F fit, L latest, C candles, P footprint.

The data

From NinjaTrader. Recorded days sign each trade against the bid and ask stored with it; the readout shows the share, and the rest fall back to the tick rule. Days built from tick history use the tick rule. Its bar imbalance correlated +0.91 with the exchange’s flag on NQ, but only about 0.5 on 6E and 6J, so read tick-rule delta with care on those. Times are UTC, from the exchange stamp, and the Lucid feed arrives about 10 minutes late. Feed: scripts/footprint/start_footprint_capture.bat.
