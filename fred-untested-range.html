Untested FRED series vs next-week range

— pre-registered 2026-11-28 Evidence Book

The question

The data catalogue held about 50 FRED series that had never been scored, including NFCI, the 5y5y forward, TIPS, breakevens, the curve legs, policy-uncertainty indices, reserves and TGA, bank credit, CP, the H.10 fixings and OVX. Does any of them, known before the week starts, say that next week's realised range (London sessions, high to low) will come in wider or narrower than the shipped range forecast? This is a size-only question. Each series is taken point-in-time: the ALFRED first release, usable only from two days after FRED published it. Two readings per series: its level and the size of its 20-day move, each as a percentile of its own trailing year. The score compares the top third with the bottom third. Discovery runs 2020-08 to 2023-08 (BH q 0.10). The held-out check, 2023-08 to 2026-08 with Holm 0.05, was never reached.

Discovery p-values against chance

If nothing were there, about 5% of cells would land under 0.05 by luck. Fewer did, and none came under 0.01. p uses a shift placebo (the signal slid against the outcome), widened by a factor measured on 300 pure-noise signals. Slow macro series line up with month-long runs in the forecast miss, and a plain bootstrap would have called dozens of those "significant".
