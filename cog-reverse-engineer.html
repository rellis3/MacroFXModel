COG Reverse-Engineer — infer his vol calc from his own outputs

We have COG's forecast levels stored per day (hl_med / hl_75 / oc_med) for EURUSD, NQ, GOLD — those are labels. On the same history strictly before each date we recompute candidate σ estimators, then ask which one (and which constant) reproduces his numbers. Three reads: (1) Ratio diagnostics — his own hl_75÷hl_med tells his percentile mapping with no fitting (ours = Feller 1.303); (2) Estimator fit — the σ whose implied constant C is most stable (lowest CV) and correlates best is likely his, and C reveals his range constant (≈1.57 = raw Feller, >1.57 = a widening); (3) Blend — the best mix of the top-2, which only wins by a wide, consistent margin (small N → a blend of many always overfits). Identifies/reproduces his line — does NOT create edge (his line already tested null as a fade). Small N: directional, not proof.
