Descriptive only, not a signal.

Unusual moves (4-week move ≥ 90th percentile of 2 years)
Unexplained: moves the textbook drivers don't cover (go read the news)
Crowded or washed-out positioning (≤ 10th or ≥ 90th percentile of 3 years)

Sign-adjusted to the market as quoted: a high percentile = long the market as quoted (e.g. long USDJPY = short yen). Fast money = leveraged funds, real money = asset managers, commodity funds = managed money.

Flipped drivers: markets that changed story
Shifting beta: how much each market moves per move in the big drivers, and whether that has changed

Beta = the market's move per driver move: % (or bp for yields) per 1% move in the Nasdaq 100, the dollar index or WTI oil, and per 10bp move in the US 10-year yield. Use it for risk, not prediction: what moves together right now, which hedges are working, where positions are one bet. Same-day links are real; this repo has found no forward edge in them. Betas from 20 sessions are noisy, and stock-bond style flips have historically lasted about a week (median 5 sessions).
