The question

When a country's 2-year government yield rises relative to the other G10 countries, the market is repricing its central bank. Does that currency keep gaining over the next 5 or 20 trading days? Each day, the 9 currencies (USD EUR JPY GBP CAD AUD CHF SEK NOK) are ranked by the 5- or 20-day change in their yield gap to the average (yields lagged one day), and that ranking is compared with how each currency then does against the basket. The score is the daily rank correlation (IC), averaged over 2005–2026. An IC of 0.03 was fixed in advance as the smallest useful effect.

What does carry information (descriptive, not scored)

The yield level (carry) ranks the next days positively and the past 20-day move ranks them negatively (short-term reversal). The yield change does not. Over the same window, yield-gap changes and currency moves line up strongly (IC +0.36 at 20 days): FX reprices with the rates market, not after it, so nothing is left to follow.
