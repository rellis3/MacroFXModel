Does the initial balance tell you where the day goes?

Steidlmayer's rule: if price breaks the first hour's range and holds outside it for an hour, follow it to the close. If it pokes out and closes back inside, fade it. We tested this with real futures volume, a flow proxy, and the 30-minute candles, against the same clock time on other days.

Each row is the effect compared with the same time and direction on other days, with its 95% interval. The dashed line is the registered pass bar (0.15σ to the close, or 0.15σ per standard deviation of flow or candle strength). A pass needs the dot past the bar, a Holm-corrected p under 0.05, and the same sign in 2016–20 and 2021–26.

TPO against order flow

Do the letters and the flow tell the same story? Over the whole history we only have a flow proxy, which infers buying and selling from each minute's price change. Because it is built from price, it agrees with the TPO by construction. The real test is the exchange's own aggressor flag: 51 NQ sessions bought from Databento (12 Jun – 21 Aug 2026).

Day types (Dalton, mechanical)

How the day ended, sorted by how far it travelled beyond the first hour. Trend and neutral-extreme days close almost a full IB width from the TPO POC. Every other type closes near it. That is a description of what happened, not something knowable at 10:30.
