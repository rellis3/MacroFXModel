The 18-line run (superseded)

  1. Lines: the 18 shadow lines (OH, OL, Close+, Close−, HL projection up and down × P50 / P75 / P90).
  2. Entry (Jordan's gold entry): price goes through a line by 10% of the stop, a stop order arms at the line, and it fills when price comes back through. Cancel if price runs 1.2× the stop past the line; chase to a further line on the same side. One trade at a time.
  3. Target: VWAP + 0.5σv (short) / VWAP − 0.5σv (long), fixed when the order fills.
  4. Stop: S1 = 0.15 × the distance from the open to the P90 line; S2 = Jordan's rolling MAE (median line-to-line MAE of that line over the last 3 months).
  5. Flat at 22:00 London. Pessimistic fills, costs on (1 tick in, 1 tick out on non-target exits, commission). In sample 2021–22, out of sample 2023 → 21 Aug 2026.
