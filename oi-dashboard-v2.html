01 Live gamma terrain 5m15m1h

How to read it: a live TradingView chart (updates every 10 s, scroll and zoom as normal). The glowing bands behind the candles are zones of dealer gamma. Green = dealers lean against moves, so price tends to stall and pin. Red = dealers push with the move, so price tends to speed up through it. Brighter = more gamma; the biggest zones are named. The curve on the right is the whole book's gamma if price were at that level — where it crosses zero is the flip.

03 When the gamma expires

Experiments — expiry map · gamma mountains · strike × expiry heat (denser, optional)

A Expiry map

Price up the side, time to expiry across on a true scale. Bubble area = share of the whole book's gamma. Shows how soon each pile bites.

B Gamma mountains

One ridge per expiry (nearest at the front), gamma along the price axis — peaks up are long gamma, dips are short. Same scale on every ridge, so a flat far-month ridge is genuinely small.

C Strike × expiry heat

Every cell is one strike in one expiry; the number is its share of the whole book's gamma. Bars on the right/top show each strike's and each expiry's total.
