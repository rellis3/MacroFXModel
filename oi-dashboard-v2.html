01 Gamma terrain move the mouse over it

Price candles trading across the dealers' gamma landscape. How to read it: the glowing bands behind the candles are zones of dealer gamma, smoothed so you see zones rather than individual strikes. Green zone = dealers lean against moves, so price tends to stall and pin there. Red zone = dealers push with the move, so price tends to speed up through it. Brighter = more gamma; the biggest zones are named on the right. The curve on the right is the whole book's total gamma if price were at that level: where it crosses zero is the regime flip, and it is the real one, recomputed at every price.

02 Expiry map

Price up the side, time to expiry across on a true scale. Bubble area = share of the whole book's gamma. Shows how soon each pile bites.

03 Gamma mountains

One ridge per expiry (nearest at the front), gamma along the price axis — peaks up are long gamma, dips are short. Same scale on every ridge, so a flat far-month ridge is genuinely small.

04 Strike × expiry heat

Every cell is one strike in one expiry; the number is its share of the whole book's gamma. Bars on the right/top show each strike's and each expiry's total.
