01 Live gamma terrain 5m15m1h

How to read it: a live TradingView chart (updates every 10 s, scroll and zoom as normal). The glowing bands behind the candles are zones of dealer gamma. Green = dealers lean against moves, so price tends to stall and pin. Red = dealers push with the move, so price tends to speed up through it. Brighter = more gamma; the biggest zones are named. The curve on the right is the whole book's gamma if price were at that level — where it crosses zero is the flip.

02 The day ahead next 1 hnext 4 h

A forecast map, not a history chart. Price runs up the side, time ahead runs across. The blue cone is where price can plausibly be (dark = half of simulated paths stay inside, light = three quarters). Each level is a line that ends in a bubble at the typical time price first touches it; a bigger, brighter bubble means a likelier touch. The bars on the right show where price tends to finish. Amber box = the range the options market is pricing.

05 Gamma by price level

The simple version. One row per price band, highest price at the top. The wide faint bar is the whole book's gamma in that band: right/green = dealers lean against moves there (price tends to stall), left/red = dealers push with the move (price tends to speed up). The slim bright bar is the part of that gamma that expires within 3 days. A long bar with a long bright centre = a big zone that is about to matter. Hover a row for numbers.
