Bennett Z — does the actual spread-reversion bot work?

The clean replication of Bennett's actual mechanism (from his dashboard): the signal is the US-vs-foreign 2Y yield-spread z-score. Enter when |z| ≥ threshold (his ±2.75) in the z-direction, betting the spread mean-reverts; exit on z-reversion to ±zExit (his ±1.5) or a max-hold. No price levels. Daily-close resolution. It also A/Bs his size-up-at-extremes rule against flat sizing, and breaks results down by z-tier — our earlier work found performance DECAYED as |z| grew, the opposite of what his sizing assumes.
