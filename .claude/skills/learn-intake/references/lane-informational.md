# Informational lane (Nicholas Crown, Jessinskip, other commentators, podcasts)

**Purpose:** market-mechanics understanding: how one market moves another, setups worth watching, and facts worth
showing on a page or alerting on. **Not** turning every clip into a trading system (owner, 2026-09-19).

## The pass (generalised from `MD files/CROWN_WATCH.md`, which stays the canonical wording)

For each claim in the clip:
1. **The actual claim**, stated as something that could be true or false, not as a vibe.
2. **What's already on the desk**: audited, not assumed (evidence_map, deskEvidence, CROWN_WATCH history, the
   Setup Watch register, `js/macroChain.js`).
3. **Trading claim or understanding claim.** Trading claims only get a prereg if the owner wants the entry tested.
   Understanding claims are checked against whether the chain on `today.html` already covers the mechanism.
4. **Display or alert nugget**, asked on every claim even if the entry is null: is there a descriptive base rate or
   "this reads as X" context line worth a page or a desk-watch trigger? Context only; never a signal.
5. **Dates are not claims** (owner, 2026-10-02). Clips arrive undated. Check "happening now" facts only to confirm the
   setup is a real configuration, never to score the presenter.

Then also ask the method question, briefly: **how did they reason** (what they looked at first, what they compared,
what would change their mind)? That goes in Approach, and into `RESEARCH_METHOD_LESSONS.md` if it's a habit worth stealing.

## Colez Trades educators: elevated pass

Max Cole, Jay, Craig and other Colez Trades educators (the wider group; C.OG is the private group inside it and has its
own lane). Owner, 2026-11-12: important, but less than C.OG. On top of the pass above:
- Every claim gets a short verbatim quote and a speaker tag; never attribute it to C.OG.
- Screen against their method as already written down (`MD files/GOLD_CHART_STRATEGY.md`, `max copier strategy.md`,
  `jay_pivot_method.md`) and what tested it (ConfluenceBot zone verdict, `vmcResearch/`, `CONFLUENCE_RESULTS.md`,
  `live-range-confluence-book`, `daily-open-fib-retest`, `delta-at-line`). Say what the item adds beyond those docs.
- Separate entry, management and bias claims; management claims are read against #27 in `RESEARCH_METHOD_LESSONS.md`.
- Self-reported P&L / drawdown / "funded" numbers are not evidence; note them as self-report.
- No second independent read (that stays C.OG-only).

## Files

- **Crown**: the note goes in `education/intake/informational/crown/`, **and** add a dated entry to `MD files/CROWN_WATCH.md`
  in its existing format (a short entry linking the note). The watch log stays the running story.
- **Other creators**: note in `education/intake/informational/<source>/`. When a creator has 5+ notes, start
  `<source>/LOG.md` in the PODCAST_INSIGHTS shape (Theme Index · Research Backlog · Calls to Score).
- **Podcasts**: keep using `education/podcast_transcripts/PODCAST_INSIGHTS.md` (PROCESS / TESTABLE / ON FILE) and
  `HOW_THEY_THINK.md`. The intake note is optional there.
- Dated market calls go to the creator's "Calls to Score" list, never into the Evidence Book.
