Reversal-Fade Test — does fading at k×median beat the median line, after cost, OOS?

The reversal-point diagnostic showed the median line is mis-placed as a reversal locus (FX turns before it, indices/gold blow through). This is the falsification test: it re-places the dynamic H-L fade line at k × the trailing median range (projected from the running intraday extreme, so it moves intrabar) and A/Bs it against k = 1 (the median line). k is per-pair, self-calibrated — estimated from that pair's own dominant reversals on the in-sample half only, then applied unchanged out-of-sample. Two exits: a confirmation-entry fade (enter on the close back through the line, stop beyond the overshoot, +30 target — dodges the blow-through tail) and a blind scalp (stop15/tgt30). Round-trip cost by type is subtracted from every trade. The verdict is the OOS row; a win = higher OOS net Sharpe than k=1 with ≥30 OOS trades. Expected prior: still null on FX — the overshoot tail is hard to beat.
