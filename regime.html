🧭 Regime

growth × inflation, monthly, on this desk's data ← Today Evidence Book
Every month gets one of four labels from two questions: is growth improving or fading, and is inflation rising or easing? Both are measured as rate of change (this quarter against the last year), from free public series, using only numbers that had printed by that month. The table below is what each market did in the month after each label — on this desk's own prices since 2007, with intervals — not a quote from a textbook. A regime is a backdrop, not a forecast: it tells you which way the current has been running, and how choppy the water usually is.
