Every kept (post concurrency-cap) trade across the selected pairs, tagged with which pair it belongs to and that pair's portfolio weight. % Returns and Currency P&L are WEIGHTED by each trade's pair weight, so summing either column reproduces the portfolio's actual combined result — not single-pair scale. R = pnlPct ÷ (stop distance, gross) is a per-trade ratio and left UNWEIGHTED (weighting both sides of a ratio cancels out). Currency P&L assumes fixed-fractional sizing scaled by pair weight: Risk($) = Account × Risk% × pair weight, PnL($) = R × Risk($).
