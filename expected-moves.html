Read this before trading off it. Cone A/B/Blend calibration is graded out-of-sample per pair by coneBlend's own walk-forward fit — that part has real evidence behind it. dayTypeScore backs live strategies elsewhere in this repo. The GEX wall read is, in its own module's words, "positioning context, not a validated signal — folklore-tier edge, partial on FX." Combining all three here is convenience, not a claim that the combination itself has been backtested or has edge. Treat every row as a starting point for your own read, not an answer. Indices specifically: Cone A's session-shape profile buckets drift/vol by UTC hour-of-day, which is tuned for FX's ~continuous trading — it hasn't been checked against index/gold-future CFDs' own daily settlement break (~22:00 UTC). It will run and produce a read, just an unvalidated one; treat index rows with more skepticism than FX until that's checked.
