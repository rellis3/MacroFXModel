Morning lines: still reached today?

What this is. At each hour the session so far is placed by how much range it has used and how fast the last hour was (both in units of the forecast's σ). The fitted history of "how much more after a session like this, at this hour" sets the projected high and low.

Evidence. Pre-registered (forge/INTRADAY_RANGE_PREREG.md): it beats the morning lines on the final day range at 21/21 hourly checkpoints for FX + gold and 19/21 for indices, out of sample. The real gain is morning to midday (FX 9–17% better 08:00–13:00); late-day gains are mostly "the day is nearly over".

Read with care. Indices' downside lines run a little tight (p75 hit 29% vs 25%). After about 15:00 the median "more to come" is usually zero, so the p50 line sits on the current extreme. Reach odds are reliable up to ~30% and understate above that. Pairs without a fit (commodities, BTC) are not listed.
