How to read this · evidence At each hour the session so far is placed by how much range it has used and how fast the last hour was, both in units of the forecast's σ. The fitted history of "how much more came after a session like this, at this hour" sets the projected high and low. Size only, never direction.
Evidence: pre-registered (forge/INTRADAY_RANGE_PREREG.md). It beats the morning lines on the final day range at 21/21 hourly checkpoints for FX + gold and 19/21 for indices, out of sample. The real gain is morning to midday (FX 9–17% better 08:00–13:00); late-day gains are mostly "the day is nearly over".
Read with care: indices' downside lines run a little tight (p75 hit 29% vs 25%). After ~15:00 the median "more to come" is usually zero, so the p50 line sits on the current extreme. Reach odds are reliable to ~30% and understate above that; beyond the fitted grid they show as <5% / >95%. Commodities and BTC have no fit and are not listed.
