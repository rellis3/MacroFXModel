Most trading books split the craft into four boxes: Fundamentals, Technicals, Behavioral, Quantitative. This page is an honest audit of where MacroFXModel actually stands in each — using the same trust icons as BACKTEST_INDEX.md (✅ proven edge · 🔁 contaminated evidence · 🔧 open defect · 🧪 context/unvalidated · 📚 infra/legacy). The point isn't to make every box look full — several are genuinely thin or absent, and that's stated plainly rather than papered over.

Method: averages up to three 0–100 inputs already computed elsewhere on the platform — vol-percentile stretch (how extended vs. its own 252-day history), HMM trend persistence (how likely the current regime is trending vs. ranging), and COT positioning crowding (how far speculative positioning sits from neutral, for whichever currency leg has CFTC coverage — indices have no clean futures-market equivalent, so positioning is left out for them rather than faked). Needs at least two inputs to show a score — anything with only one is marked "not enough inputs" rather than guessed. This is a fresh, unvalidated synthesis (🧪) — it has not been backtested, and a high score means "several reinforcing signals are stacked right now," not "trade this."

Genuine gaps, in plain terms

Fundamentals is structurally thin, not broken. This platform trades FX, gold, and index CFDs — not individual stocks — so there's no earnings data, no company financials, no valuation models to build. That's a description of what this platform is, not a missing feature.

Behavioral is the weakest quadrant. Sentiment and Positioning are real, live data — but neither has been proven to predict price; they're always context, never a signal to act on alone. Cognitive Bias and Bubbles & Crashes exist only as teaching material today — no tool watches your own trading for bias, and no detector flags mania/panic conditions live.

Technicals and Quantitative carry the platform. That's not an accident — this is where the real, tested edge lives (Range-Line above all), and where the infrastructure to prove or kill an idea (backtesting, cost modeling, OOS discipline) is genuinely rigorous.
