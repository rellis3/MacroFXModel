⚖ Fade vs Follow — what separates them?
For every touch of an armed line we race a symmetric move from the touch bar — revert one inner-band (fade) vs continue the same distance out (follow) — then bucket the outcome by candidate signals. Fade edge = Revert% − Continue% (+ green = reverts won, − red = continues won). In-sample, causal (read at/after the touch), exploratory — not an edge claim. Prior M1 testing (5 pairs) came back ~null with vol-spent running opposite to the exhaustion premise; this checks it on the current range.
