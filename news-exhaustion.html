News-Exhaustion — does the economic calendar predict fade vs follow?

The one ex-ante, exogenous, non-circular conditioning signal — the calendar is known before the session opens. Thesis (near-replicated): high-impact Major news (NFP/CPI/FOMC/central-bank) drives volatility expansion + continuation — the trend day that blows through the 75th (you follow); quiet none sessions are the range days that revert (you fade). Each session is bucketed by its in-session news for the pair's currencies, then measured per bucket, IS/OOS. Honest split: the classifier (news → more continuation) is likely to show; whether the after-cost fade/follow edge beats the unconditional baseline is harder — news is exactly when spreads are widest. The numbers separate them.
