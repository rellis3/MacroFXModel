The primary signal: what decides direction, and what the vol forecast is for
explainer · 2026-11-06 · built from the Evidence Book, the direction bar and C.OG's "forecaster is one layer of six" (Discord, 2026-09-28)
In one line. The primary signal answers "which way, and why must someone move price that way?". The vol forecast answers "how far, how fast, how much to risk". They are separate questions with separate evidence. Every attempt here to get the first answer out of the second has failed, which is exactly what C.OG said would happen: "it's not quite possible to match what I'm doing by using purely a vol forecaster".

1. The layers, and where each question is answered

C.OG: "having the forecaster is very useful as a layer … the way to really develop this is by having conditional views and then other layers supporting it." The stop orders Jordan wanted to copy come from layers 1, 3 and 4 together; that is why they can't be reverse-engineered from the lines.

2. What a primary signal has to be

  • A reason someone must trade. Carry (paid to hold), forced hedging, index or month-end rebalancing, a central bank repricing the path, a crowded position unwinding. "Price touched a line" is not a reason; nobody is forced to act there.
  • Sparse. Real edges here are rare conditions that move the odds a lot, not a daily call. Most days the honest answer is "no view".
  • Scored against its own base rate. NQ closed up on 55% of days and 65% of 20-day windows. "Long NQ" looks clever against 50% and has no skill against drift.
  • A horizon that matches its mechanism. Carry plays out over months, post-FOMC drift over five sessions. A slow reason can't be traded on an intraday stop.

3. Why the vol forecast can't be it

  • σ has no sign. The lines are symmetric widths around the open; they say how big, never which way.
  • Touches are generic. Lines of the same size built from plain ATR give the same results at every rung (fade, follow, VWAP stretch, targets).
  • Six NQ tests this week: open-long to p75/p90, fade/follow at the lines, v3 lines, p50 follow on 2017–20 (did not replicate), VWAP stretch. All drift or noise. Day reconstruction M1–M7 found the same on the production ladder.
  • What it does well: it measures size well (v3 cuts range error ~12%), reach odds, how much range is left, and stop distance (price overshoots a line by a median 0.3–0.5σ).

5. How the pieces combine (already built)

The desk's macro analysis already feeds one combiner: Global Read. Each input carries a weight from its evidence (tier × measured IC). Inputs that tested null get weight 0, so they show as context but cannot move the call. The result is turned into P(up) and then into a 1-to-10 direction score, measured in units of what the data can detect.

  • P(up) = base rate + the shift from tested inputs
  • 8–10 = long, 1–3 = short, 4–7 = no direction (most days)
  • Every score is written to the forward direction ledger and scored against the base rate

Adding more null inputs would only add noise. The old conviction vote (every input counted equally) was anti-predictive: the bot lost about £38k on it.

6. How you'd know a primary signal works

The direction bar (31 Oct) measured how much a signal must move the odds of an up close before the record can see it. For one market, with the signal firing on 10% of days:

HorizonNeeded shift in P(up)
1 day6 points
5 days15 points
20 days29 points

So: pool across markets or events, prefer sparse large-effect conditions, and let the forward record run. No backtest alone can prove a small edge here. Meta-labelling only helps once a primary signal has an edge. Our four meta-label tests were null because the primary they filtered had none.

7. How to use it day to day

  • Direction: read the direction score. If it's 4–7 there is no tested reason to pick a side. A trade then is a discretionary view, not a system call, and should be sized like one.
  • Stop, target, size: from the vol layer. Put the stop beyond the line plus the overshoot (about 0.35σ). The target is a line with a known reach probability. Size so 1R equals the stop distance. Size down when the forecast has been missing (the stress shadow lines are wider).
  • Don't chase beyond p75/p90 in the move's direction; only about 0.3σ of travel is typically left.
  • Trade plan page (trade-plan.html): joins the direction score and the vol layer into one card per market and keeps a forward record. Vol state (/api/vol-state): C.OG's shared state variables in one place.
What would change this page. A direction cell passing its forward judgement in the ledger (post-FOMC drift pooled across central banks is the nearest). FX carry reaching power as months accumulate. A new mechanism with a clear "who must trade". Not more combinations of inputs already tested null.
