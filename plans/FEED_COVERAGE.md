# Feed coverage register

Generated by analysis/feed_coverage.mjs from js/feedCoverage.js. Do not edit by hand. Guard test: js/feedCoverage.test.mjs.

The worry this answers: good feeds get built and then forgotten, and never reach the newsletter. CFTC positioning was the example. It existed for months and was only a one-line currency card mention until it got its own section.

A feed counts as used only if its data reaches a field that js/newsletter.js reads. That was traced from atlas.html through nlInput, nlStrips, nlExtras and nlHmm into the newsletter code, and the weekly branches were checked one by one. Where a trace was not possible the feed is marked as not used and the register says so.

Institutional means primary data a macro desk or fund would pay for or treat as primary: CFTC positioning, CME settlements and implied vol, the Cboe vol family, listed options open interest and dealer gamma, STIR futures, central bank statements, ALFRED vintage surprises, BIS statistics, ICE BofA spreads, nowcasts and official auctions and calendars.

Read the daily and weekly columns with care. Almost every used feed appears in both editions, because the two issues share one builder. Only the weekly ranges and the Nasdaq forward calendar are weekly only, and the hourly market-state strip is daily only.

## Recommended next wiring

Ranked by value. Every item respects the ledger: ranges, size, who must act and plain context only. None of them is a direction signal.

1. IV expected move beside the typical day on each market card. The ledger id iv-forecast is validated: implied vol from CME settlements beat realised vol models. The range forecast already prints a typical day, so this is one more number on the same card. Source is /api/forecast-path/iv.
2. Band reach odds in the daily issue. band-reach-from-here is validated, and the issue already says how much of a normal day is used. A line saying how likely the far edge is from here fits the same bar. Source is /api/forecast-path/reach.
3. One line on crowded funds on high impact release rows. positioning-into-release-direction is validated as who gets hurt, not which way price goes. The crowding data and the release rows are both already in the issue, so this joins two things the reader sees apart. Word it as who must act.
4. The nowcast in The bigger picture. nlInput already builds n.nowcast and the newsletter never reads it. The GDP gap is context and the CPI gap is null, so show the level and say it is context.
5. Printed results from the last day on release rows. The calendar feed with actuals is loaded by Atlas and dropped. Showing what printed against forecast is data, not a signal, and the surprise score is already on the currency cards.
6. A weekly backdrop line from the funding and leverage read. Every state is a description of where a number sits in its own history. The funding stress test is null, so keep it to one weekly sentence marked context.

## What this register does not cover

This register is built from the Atlas CORE and EXTRA feed lists, the server routes and the data feed map, so it covers what a trader would care about and skips admin, bot, backtest, job and cache routes. It is not the full inventory: the data catalogue admits it is incomplete, and about 40 FRED series are pulled and never tested. A feed that is not in this list is a gap in the register, not proof that the desk has no such feed. When you add a feed to Atlas, add it here in the same commit or the guard test fails.
