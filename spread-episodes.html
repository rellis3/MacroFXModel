What was tested

Two separate studies, pre-registered (forge/SPREAD_EPISODES_PREREG.md) before any outcome was computed. Case 1: SOFR Sep26 rate − Euribor Mar27 rate (bp, 15-min) against NAS100. Case 2: the average of the US 2Y and 10Y yields (daily) against EURUSD. A spread turn is the bar of its running extreme; it is confirmed when the spread has moved back by k × its trailing volatility. Everything is measured from the confirmation, using only data available at that time. "Follow" means price moved the way the trailing same-bar link says it should (≥ 0.5σ), "revert" the opposite way (≤ −0.5σ). Split: discover → validate → final period, opened once. Controls: matched random bars, the same spread shifted by random days, and a price-only model.
