Reversal-Point Research — where does price actually reverse?

A separate calc from the vol forecast. Instead of "how far does price travel" (a range σ), this extracts the intraday swings that held and reversed (the real exhaustion points) and measures them against the same anchors: the London-midnight open (O-C) and the running intraday extreme (dynamic H-L). The key column is Run P50 vs Median range — if reversals typically run less than the median range before turning, the median line sits further out than where price actually exhausts. Full M1 history. A reversal = a swing ≥ revFrac × the pair's median daily range.
Two lenses below the main table: Dominant vs Minor separates the day's ultimate high+low swings (the tradeable turn) from intraday pullbacks — so the "reverses early" signal isn't just noise-swing clutter. Forecast-zone occupancy buckets every reversal by where it exhausted relative to the two forecast lines: short of the median (k<1), in the median→75th shelf (1–1.30×), or past the 75th (≥1.30× — the overshoot tail).
