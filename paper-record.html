Rich-Vol Break Record paper forward test, no orders

The one rule that passed its pre-registered tests in the Fade/Continue research: on days when implied vol is rich versus realised vol, take the first 1-minute close at least 0.05σ through a Vol Forecast line between 00:00 and 10:00 London, entering at the next bar's open in the break direction. Stop 0.1σ or 0.2σ behind the line, targets 5R and 10R, exit at the day's end. Each trade's R is the average of those four variants, net of spread. Backtest: +0.118R per trade on FX/gold, +0.071R on indices, but most trades lose 1R and a few large winners carry the result, so it needs a few hundred trades before the live number means much. Rich flags are set the evening before (from 21:00 London) from the options capture's implied vol (FX, gold) or the CBOE vol index (NQ VXN, SPX VIX, DOW VXD, US2000 RVX) ÷ 20-day realised vol.
