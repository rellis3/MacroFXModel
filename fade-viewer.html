Fade Viewer — one day, the forecast, and exactly what the engine did

Pick a pair + date and see the session's forecast bands (median/75th), the 1-min candle path, and the engine's read of every level: was it touched, what did WaveTrend + VWAP say at the touch, did it confirm a fade, and where did the managed exit land. This is the trust tool — check by eye that the engine finds the fades the way you do. Same inspectSession brick as the validated backtest (byte-identical to detectSessionSignals). Data comes from the M1 parquet snapshot; days past its end (incl. yesterday) are gap-filled live from OANDA on demand — a LIVE tag shows when that happened.
