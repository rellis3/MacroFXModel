Numeric composition, not an AI text read: each dimension is scored independently (-1 weak to +1 strong) against its own trailing history, then combined into a strength composite from payrolls + hiring breadth + unemployment + participation — wage growth and revision surprise are shown separately, not blended in, since fast wage growth is a strong-labor signal AND an inflation-risk signal at once, and a revision is a verdict on the PRIOR month's print, not this one. "Hiring breadth" is BLS's own diffusion-index framing (% of the 10 major industry sectors adding jobs, this month's) plus which single sector carried the most of the net change. "Revision surprise" compares the current, most-revised payrolls figure against what was FIRST reported for that same month (FRED's vintage/ALFRED data), so a print that looked weak on release day but got revised up shows up here. USD has full coverage; the other 7 currencies are unemployment-trend-only for now — see the note on each card. Refreshes automatically once/day; source is FRED (needs FRED_KEY on the server).
