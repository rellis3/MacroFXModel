How to read this

Bonds are the reservoir, credit the pipes, FX the valves, equities the tap. Each link is one textbook relationship judged on measured 20-day moves at both ends. Holding: the usual mechanism is doing the pricing. Broken: both ends moved, the wrong way round, and that mismatch is the story. Quiet: at least one end has not moved past its noise floor, so it is not judged.
