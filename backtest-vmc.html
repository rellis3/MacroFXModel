Backtest VMC test — does exhaustion confirm the fades?

The backtestSystem bot fades levels, but its confidence vote is built from trend features and turned out mildly anti-predictive (higher conviction lost more). Hypothesis: a level fade should be confirmed by exhaustion — fade support only when WaveTrend is oversold, fade resistance only when overbought. This reads causal VMC (operator's 9/12/3) at each historical entry and buckets outcomes by VMC agreement. ← Dashboard
How to read it. If confirm (VMC agreed with the fade) has a materially higher win-rate / expectancy than oppose/neutral, VMC is a candidate entry gate worth validating out-of-sample. If not, it isn't — and we don't force it. This is exploratory: n≈279 over one ~2-month window (Jun–Jul 2026), so a bucket under 30 trades is "no conclusion", and even a clean split here is a steer, not proof until confirmed OOS. Gross R is netted for cost. Losses cap at ~1R (the stop works) — this is about which fades to take, not exits.
