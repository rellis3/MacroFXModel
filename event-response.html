Which releases the market actually reacts to

The 30-minute window around the release, divided by the same clock on ordinary days — so 3× means the half-hour of the print moved three times a normal half-hour. Anything at or below 1× is a release the tape does not register at all. Bars past the amber line clear the 2× bar this book uses to call a reaction real.

The most similar setups before this one

The eight prior events whose pre-event state — 5-session moves in the 2y, 10y, 30y, the 10y TIPS real yield, the 10y breakeven and the curve slope — most resembles the latest one, and what followed each. The real-yield and breakeven series are stored to two decimals, so their five-session changes quantise to whole basis points — those two columns are coarser than the nominals, which is a limit of the source, not of the matching. This is a lookup, not a prediction. Tested as a predictor (EVENT_RESPONSE_BOOK.md §7, walk-forward, k=8) it hit 49.3% — and a placebo with the state shuffled scored 51.5%, i.e. matching on the real state did worse than ignoring it.
