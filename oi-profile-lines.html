Do the OI Profile lines hold or break?

Every morning of our own OI capture since 20 August, rebuilt through the live OI Profile and scored against that day's price. Each line is compared with the same line shifted ±0.25σ and ±0.5σ, so the question is whether the OI line matters, not whether price reacts to touches in general.

Method

Capture: oi_raw_<date> (QuikStrike, ~06:40 UTC), rebuilt with buildOiProfile exactly as /api/oi-profile/export (strongest-expiry book, ±3σ window, that day's forecast σ). Lines are scored from the capture time to 22:00 London on OANDA M1. A line counts as touched once price has closed 0.25σ away and then reaches it; touch 2, 3… need a fresh 0.25σ move away first. Uncertainty: bootstrap over dates (all instruments of a day together), B = 2000. A random-walk control returned ~0 with nothing planted and +10.9pp with a planted bounce, so the test can see an effect of that size.
