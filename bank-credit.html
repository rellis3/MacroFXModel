What the evidence allows

UNDERPOWEREDbank-credit-dollar-dxy: dollar-credit growth vs the next quarter's dollar index, r −0.10 [−0.24, +0.02], 165 quarters.

CONTEXTbank-credit-dollar-nasdaq: r −0.16 [−0.28, −0.06] against the next Nasdaq quarter, but it rests on the 2000 and 2008 busts. Without them r is −0.07, and in the 2020s it is +0.12. The fast-growth tercile (above 7.6% a year) averaged −0.5% the next quarter, against +4.6% in the slow tercile.

So this page shows a regime: how fast leverage is building, and where. It is not a dollar or Nasdaq call, and nothing sizes on it. See the Evidence Book and forge/BANK_CREDIT_DOLLAR_PREREG.md.
