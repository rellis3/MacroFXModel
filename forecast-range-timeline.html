Forge Range Timeline — forecast vs realized, day by day (not a single-date snapshot)

Range accuracy ≠ volatility exhaustion — this shows whether the forecast's SIZE tracked reality over a stretch of days (Panel A's question, from forecast-accuracy.html, made sequential); it says nothing about where price turns (Panel B / volatilityExhaustion/, a separate question). Data is a static walk-forward OOS export (forge/vol.py → forge/export_vol_compare.py) — read-only, independent of the live forecaster's own KV state. Forecast for day t only ever used data through day t−1 (see forge/vol.py's oos_predictions and its causality test).
