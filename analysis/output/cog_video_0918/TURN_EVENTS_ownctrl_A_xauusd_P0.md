### Limits (stated, not hidden)
- Only the pinned pair has deep history, so contract-month robustness is NOT tested; other pairs hold two weeks.
- No period is untouched: earlier studies in this repository saw these dates for the combined line. The honest untouched test is the forward record from 2026-10-15.
- MFE/MAE use 15-minute closes (no intra-bar high/low in the Nasdaq series).
- Strata are session x volatility tercile by the line-turn confirm time.
- Release exclusion uses a fixed 08:30/10:00 ET (all weekdays) and 14:00 ET (FOMC days) rule, not an event vendor calendar.
