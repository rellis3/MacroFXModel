
## A2 (POST-HOC): beta conditioned on the HEDGE leg's days, not on A's

Median beta over 250-day windows every 21 days. Conditioning on the hedge leg does not shrink the slope mechanically. "Worst" = the hedge leg's worst 25% days in the window, "best" = its best 25%, "big move" = its largest 25% by absolute move.
