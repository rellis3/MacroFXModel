Prereg: `forge/HEDGE_METHOD_COMPARISON_PREREG.md` (registered in `ab98087b`, amendment A1 in `914d4a84`, both before the run). Run 2026-11-20, seed 20261120, B = 500, 21-day blocks.

Full tables: `RESULTS_TABLES.md` (generated by `run.mjs`), `RESULTS_A2.md` (post-hoc, `h2b.mjs`), `results.json`, `h2b.json`.

## Verdict: VALIDATED for one method, by a small margin; the other methods are equal or worse

Sample: 2,701 common daily closes 2016-01-04 → 2026-08-20 from M1 (NY-close days), 12 fixed target/hedge problems, walk-forward from 2016-12-23 (2,451 out-of-sample days). Every hedge ratio for day *t* used returns up to *t−1* only.

**What the registered gate (H1) found, against the Desk Terminal's method (OLS, 60 days):**

- **EWMA least squares (λ = 0.97) clears the gate:** +1.10pp of variance removed [+0.62, +1.71], better in 12 of 12 problems, in both halves (+0.71 / +1.56), survives BH at q = 0.10, and beats the placebo. 27.1% → 28.2% pooled. Reliable, but small: it is an extra pound-in-a-hundred of risk removed, not a different order of hedge.
- **Blend (half OLS60, half OLS250)** is just under the bar: +0.97pp [+0.43, +1.47], 11 of 12 problems, both halves positive. It misses the 1.0pp bar by 0.03pp, so by the registered rule it is UNDERPOWERED, not validated. Read it as the same finding as EWMA.
- **OLS 120d, OLS 250d, ridge, Kalman:** all within about ±1pp of OLS60 (intervals straddle zero). Nothing to choose between them on variance removed. Longer windows and the Kalman filter trade slightly less tail protection for much lower turnover (Kalman 0.002/day vs 0.022 for OLS60).
- **Reliably worse than OLS60:** naive unit hedge (−100pp: it ignores the volatility ratio, so it adds risk on 7 of 12 problems), downside-beta weighting (−7.1pp, see the flaw below) and minimum-expected-shortfall (−2.8pp, 1 of 12 better). Tuning the hedge to the tail on 250 days of data costs more than it gains.

**What matters far more than the method is the pair.** Variance removed ranges from about 0% (US2000 ← gold) to 60% (NQ ← US2000, EURUSD ← USDCHF) while the best-versus-worst method gap on a pair is 1–5pp. The pooled placebo (OLS60 against a random partner from the rest of the panel, which includes near-twins) removes 25.3% against 27.1% for the chosen pairs, so picking the partner by hand adds little over a random draw from a pool that already holds close relatives. Gold against DXY removes about 20% whichever way it is sized; NQ against gold and USDJPY removes 5–8%.

## Controls

Placebo partner (OLS60, random partner outside the problem's set, 50 draws, median): 25.3% pooled variance removed. Block bootstrap uses the same date blocks for every problem and method. Weights verified to use only returns before the day they hedge (code read, and the Kalman state is read before it is updated).

## Deviations from the prereg

- A1 (pre-results): problem 5 changed from SPX ← GOLD to US2000 ← GOLD (SPX M1 starts 2021); min-ES weights refresh every 5 days; Kalman scales and placebo pool defined.
- **A2 (post-hoc, results seen): a design flaw in H2 and in method M8.** Conditioning on A's own worst days selects on the *dependent* variable, which shrinks an OLS slope toward zero even when the relationship is perfectly linear. The pre-registered H2 therefore reads "downside beta is about half the full beta" for every pair (ratio 0.34–0.70), which is a statistical artefact, not a finding, and M8 (built the same way) is mechanically too small, which is why it did worst. The corrected, descriptive version conditions on the **hedge leg's** days (RESULTS_A2.md).

## Descriptive (not part of the verdict)

Beta conditioned on the hedge leg's bad days barely moves from the all-days beta: gold ← DXY −1.02 vs −0.89 (hedge leg at its worst); EURUSD ← USDCHF −0.75 vs −0.69; GBPUSD ← EURUSD 0.79 vs 0.81; AUDUSD ← NQ 0.18 vs 0.19; DE30 ← NQ 0.54 vs 0.50; NQ ← US2000 0.72 vs 0.92 (+27%, the largest shift). So the suspicion that the hedge ratio sized on average days is too small on the days that matter is **not** supported here for most pairs; it holds weakly for the index pair, and gold against NQ/US2000 are too noisy to read. Correlations tightening in a sell-off was not visible in daily betas for these 10 pairs over 2016–2026.

## What this changes

- The Hedges tab can say a recency-weighted (EWMA) ratio removed about 1pp more variance than the 60-day ratio, out of sample, on 12 of 12 test pairs, and that longer or fancier methods add nothing reliable. It should keep the 60-day OLS as the headline (simple, and within about 1pp) and show EWMA beside it.
- **The out-of-sample column is the useful addition:** the tab's in-window r² should sit next to the variance that pair actually removed on the following days.
- A hedge tuned to the tail (min-ES, downside-weighted) should not be offered as a better hedge.
- Reopen with options-implied or intraday betas, the stress-day set conditioned on a driver (VIX, real yields) rather than on the hedge leg, and WTI/silver/copper once they have a long M1 history.
