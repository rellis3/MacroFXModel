Expiry map — preview (real data, mock layout)

Price runs up the side (same axis as your price chart). Across is time to expiry on a true scale — today on the left, the far months on the right — so you see how soon each pile of gamma bites. Bubble area = share of the whole book's gamma; green = long gamma (pins), red = short gamma (amplifies). The amber band is the near-dated zone (≤3 DTE), the percentages under each expiry say how much of the book lives there.
