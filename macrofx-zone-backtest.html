The A/B is the test. All four modes trade the same way; they differ only in the gate and the direction rule:
• zone — the MacroFXModel: only zones where ≥ min distinct sources agree; direction by the state selector.
• isolated — the null: same selector, confluence gate dropped (any single level trades). Claim A = zone must beat isolated OOS.
• zone_fade / zone_follow — always fade / always follow at zones. Claim B = zone (adaptive) must beat the better of these OOS.
"Worked" = zone beats isolated and beats the best fixed rule on OOS Sharpe, Sharpe > 0, at ≥30 OOS trades. Anything less — in-sample-only, <30 OOS trades, or Sharpe inside its own ± error bar of zero — has shown nothing.
