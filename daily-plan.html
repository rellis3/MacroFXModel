Used the range so far as a share of today's p75 range (red bar = past it)
Room left Live Range's re-forecast at the last hour: more range still likely to come (p50 · p75). Mostly set by the time of day (range used and pace add ~1–1.5%); in FX it runs ~3 pts wide at p75
Stop / size for a long and a short entered now: the nearest stop a single 5-min bar rarely jumps (≤ 5% of days), and the size whose planned loss, including the worst-case step past the stop, equals your budget
What this page will not tell you
Which way to trade. Every honest test of direction from these lines, and of meta-labelling a directional book on them, came back empty (Evidence Book). The lines say how far price can travel and how much to hold.
Size is in units of the instrument per your budget in its quote currency (EURUSD: euros per USD of budget). Convert yourself for cross-currency accounts.

Where each part comes from · the lessons Lines: the chosen forecast, picked head-to-head over all five daily forecasts (forge/FORECAST_PICK_PREREG.md): the Forecast's σ nudged for regime, recent range misses and weekday (Lesson 03 §02, persistence), plus implied vol on the 13 instruments that have it. Walk-forward 2020–2026: 0.964 of the plain Forecast where IV exists, 0.980 elsewhere; stable under parameter nudges (forge/STABILITY_AND_HOLDOUT_PREREG.md). Holdout sealed until 2027-01-04.
Room left: Live Range (forge/INTRADAY_RANGE_PREREG.md). The side study found it behaves mostly as a clock (forge/LIVE_RANGE_CLOCK_BASELINE_PREREG.md) and sits ~3 pts too wide at p75 in FX because its grids were fitted on a slightly lower σ (Evidence Book: live-range-sigma-basis). Read it as "how much day is left", not as an exhaustion detector.
Stop and size: forge/HOW_MUCH_SPEC.md (Lesson 03 §03: jump risk passes through stops; Lesson 01 §06: size below Kelly). Minimum stop 0.55–0.60σ by class; the worst-case step past it is a cautious upper bound.
Not here: entry signals. Direction at the lines and two meta-labelling builds were tested properly and closed (forge/META_LABEL_PROPER_PREREG.md).
