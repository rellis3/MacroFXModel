Trade plan shadow: which way (direction score) × how far (vol forecast)
trade-plan-shadow · pre-registered 2026-11-06 · display only, not a signal, no bot reads this · results in R, never money
How this is built. Two layers, kept apart. Which way comes only from the tested direction evidence: the 1-to-10 direction score as of the last New York close (8–10 long, 1–3 short, 4–7 no trade). That evidence mostly says no direction, so most days show NO TRADE, which is the correct answer, not a fault. How far comes from the vol forecast: the stop sits 0.35σ beyond the opposite-side p90 line (price overshoots a line by a median 0.3–0.5σ on NQ), the target is the same-side p75 line, and 1R is the stop distance, so size = risk budget ÷ stop distance. Forecast error widens the lines: when the stress-shadow factor (the validated forecast-miss feedback) exists for a market, the stop uses those wider lines. On no-trade days both sides are shown greyed as a reference ("if you had a view"). Each session is written once to a forward record and scored on 1-minute bars against the same plan with a coin-flip side and, for NQ/S&P/gold, buy-and-hold.
