The question (Jordan's "jackets" test)

On each of the 18 shadow lines (OH, OL, Close+, Close−, HL projection up and down, each at P50 / P75 / P90) we fade the first touch of the day and measure the MAE line to line: from the touch until price reaches the line inside, the next line out (MAE capped there) or 22:00 London.

Today's stop = the median MAE over the last N sessions, for N = 1 day … 12 months. A window is good if that stop lands close to what the next month's median MAE turns out to be. "Miss" is how far off it was, as a % of the truth. In-sample 2021–22, out-of-sample 2023 → 21 Aug 2026.
