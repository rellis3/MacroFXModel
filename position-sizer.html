Position Sizer — vol-based sizing off the forecast (the risk layer)

Where the durable edge actually lives: vol-based sizing + diversification + a stop outside the noise. This turns the (calibrated) forecast range into position sizes that risk the same $ on every trade regardless of the pair's volatility, and caps the book's total heat. It is a risk framework, not an alpha source — it doesn't create edge, it makes whatever entry you use survivable and equal-risk. The core is currency-agnostic (keyed off the forecast range as % of price), so there's no pip/FX-rate conversion bug: notional = riskPct ÷ stop%, a stop-out loses exactly your risk. Wider forecast range ⇒ smaller position, automatically. Range comes from the live vol forecast, tightened by the calibrated factor (Forecast Accuracy build) so it's the accurate range, not the wide one.
