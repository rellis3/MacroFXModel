How to read this honestly. Cone A assumes a lognormal step process (per-bar EWMA σ + session-shaped drift, same math as forecast-path.html). Cone B makes no distributional assumption: it finds every prior bar in the same (trend regime × realized-vol) state as right now and uses the empirical spread of what price actually did next as the envelope — reused causal classifiers (classifyRegime, rollingPercentile), not new regime logic. Blend is quantile averaging in log-return space (Vincentization), weighted by each cone's trailing pinball loss — practically "Bayesian model averaging in spirit," not a literal posterior-weighted mixture of two likelihoods (this repo doesn't have the live-tracked data yet to fit that honestly). The weight is looked up by Cone B's own (regime, vol-bucket) read when that bucket has graded enough windows, else it falls back to one global weight — a thin bucket is not trusted with its own number. Every calibration number below is out-of-sample: weights are fit on the first slice of history, then blend / Cone A / Cone B are all graded on the remainder they never saw — the blend only earns the extra machinery if it beats both cones there, not in-sample. Not yet built: a replay slider, a persisted forward-track (live post-hoc record), and the third/fourth cone legs (OU reversion timing, options-implied density) discussed as later upgrades — this is the two-cone version, shipped and graded before anything gets added to it.
