Path Map — how price behaves around the forecast lines

Layer 4 of the Forecaster system. A map, not a trade. For every first touch of a HAR ladder line (OH/OL p50, p75, p90), what came first afterwards: the next line out (continuation) or the level back in (fade: p50→open, p75→p50, p90→p75)? Each share is compared with a random walk started at the line, which reaches the next line first with probability b ÷ (a + b) from its own distances. Above that is continuation, below is fading. A cell is called a dynamic only if it beats both the random walk and its rung's average by more than 2 standard errors, the same way, in both halves of the sample.
