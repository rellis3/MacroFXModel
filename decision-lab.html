What this page is. A workbench beside the locked lessons record (tag lesson-track-L05), which it reads but never changes. It takes every rule A trade (the rich-day break on the chosen v3 lines, 11 instruments, 2020-08 → 2026-08, checked on 1-minute bars) and adds what was knowable at the entry minute from the feature store: interest rates, the 10-year yield gap, COT positioning, VIX and its term structure, US data surprises, IV÷RV, time and day.
Nothing here is evidence yet. Every split and filter you try is another look at the same 2,057 trades, and some will look good by luck. The counter below keeps score of how many you have tried. The page opens on 2020–23 so the later years stay unseen while you look for ideas; look at 2024–26 only to check an idea you have already written down. Ideas worth keeping go into one registered test (draft: forge/DECISION_LAB_FILTERS_PREREG.md), found on 2020–23 and checked on 2024–26, before they count.

The chance band is the spread of the "top third minus bottom third" gap when the trade results are shuffled 300 times: a gap outside it is unusual by chance alone, but with nine inputs on the table about one in twenty will land outside by luck. "+" on a signed input means it was on the trade's side (e.g. carry earned by the trade, rates moving its way, funds already positioned its way).
