What this is

System blueprints · papers that fit together

Each card combines several papers into one system: one paper gives the direction (or a forecast), and the others add a regime filter, timing, sizing, an exit or a cost model on the same market and horizon. Every card was checked against the Evidence Book. A red chip means the desk already tested that part and found nothing; the card then says what the combination adds. These are leads for a prereg, not results. Click a paper title to open it below.

What the library holds · by mechanism family

Groups of papers on the same effect

Papers with the same family, role, market and horizon. The more strong (A/B) papers in a group, the better established the effect is in the literature, which is not the same as working on our data.
